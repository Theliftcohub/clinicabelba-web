## How to Prepare for Your Breast Surgery in Barcelona: Step-by-Step

We guide you step-by-step to ensure your breast surgery is completely calm and safe.

**Preoperative consultation and necessary tests**

During the first visit, we evaluate your anatomy, breast tissue type, expectations, and overall health. We take precise measurements and perform 3D simulations. Tests are requested: a complete blood count, electrocardiogram, and mammogram if you are over 35 years old or have a family history.

**Instructions in the days prior to surgery**

2 weeks prior: stop smoking, suspend anticoagulants (aspirin, ibuprofen), and supplements like vitamin E or omega-3. 48 hours prior: fast from midnight the day before, prepare comfortable front-opening clothing, and arrange for a companion for discharge.

**The day of surgery: what to expect**

Arrive at the clinic 1 hour prior. Final interview with the anesthesiologist, personalized surgical marking, and preparation. The surgery lasts between 60-90 minutes under general anesthesia. Discharge on the same day or after an overnight observation period, depending on your case.

### Required documentation

* National Identity Document
* Health card or private health insurance
* Results of requested lab work and tests
* List of usual medications, if any

## Frequently Asked Questions about Breast Augmentation in Barcelona

Resolve your most common questions about breast augmentation surgery

## The technique

### Breast Implants or Fat Transfer?

The choice between breast implants and fat grafting (augmentation with your own fat) fundamentally depends on how much volume you want to gain and how much fat you have available in other areas. Breast implants allow for increases of 1 to 4 cup sizes with a predictable and long-lasting result, and are the most versatile option. Augmentation with your own fat is completely natural—without foreign bodies—but is limited to gains of 1 to 2 cup sizes and requires sufficient donor fat. Clínica Belba's hybrid technique combines both to achieve the best of both worlds: the volume of implants and the naturalness of your own fat.

### Do breast implants have an expiration date?

State-of-the-art modern implants (Motiva, Mentor) do not have an established expiration date. With a lifetime manufacturer's warranty, they do not need to be replaced if there are no complications. Periodic check-ups ensure their correct condition. For more information, you can read this article about [Breast implants typically last 10 to 20 years.](https://clinicabelba.com/cuanto-duran-protesis-pecho/)

### Can I breastfeed after breast augmentation?

Yes, in most cases. The approach (inframammary or axillary) and the technique used preserve the milk ducts. In the pre-operative consultation, we evaluate your specific case and plan the surgery while preserving lactation function.

## The process

### What is the pre-operative consultation like?

The preoperative consultation is one of the most important moments of the process. During this consultation, the patient's anatomy, skin quality, breast position, body proportions, and desired outcome are assessed. Aspects such as the type of implant, volume, placement plane, surgical approach, and necessary pre-surgery tests are also defined. The goal is not just to choose a size, but to design a safe, proportionate procedure that is consistent with each patient's body.

### How long does the operation last?

Breast augmentation surgery usually lasts between 60 and 90 minutes, although this can vary depending on the technique used and if it is combined with other procedures. In many cases, it is performed as an outpatient surgery or with a short hospital stay, always depending on the immediate recovery after the procedure and medical assessment.

### What anesthesia is used?

It is usually performed under general anesthesia to ensure comfort, control, and safety throughout the surgery. In some specific cases, other options may be considered, but the most common approach for breast augmentation is to perform the procedure with the patient asleep. The definitive indication is always determined by the surgical and anesthesiology team after the preoperative evaluation.

## Recovery

### How long does breast augmentation recovery take?

Initial recovery is usually relatively quick, although the full process is progressive. Many patients can resume a gentle routine within a few days, but full recovery typically takes around 4 to 6 weeks, depending on the type of surgery, the implant placement, and individual progress. During this time, it's normal for the breasts to change in appearance until they settle.

### What type of bra is used post-surgery?

After surgery, a post-surgical or compression bra is usually recommended. It's designed to support the breasts, reduce swelling, and promote proper tissue adaptation during the initial weeks. The exact type of bra and duration of use depend on each individual case and the surgeon's instructions.

### When are the definitive results seen?

Although the change is noticeable from the beginning, the final result is not appreciated immediately. Initially, there may be swelling, the breast may appear somewhat high or firmer than expected, and this is part of the normal process. The usual course is for the result to refine itself over weeks and months, until the tissues relax and the breast takes on its final shape.

## Results and scars

### Does breast augmentation leave a visible scar?

Yes, all surgery leaves a scar, but in a well-planned breast augmentation, the goal is to make it as discreet as possible. The most common places to put it are in less visible areas, such as the inframammary fold, around the areola, or in some cases, in the armpit. Initially, the scar may appear more noticeable, but it usually improves with time and often becomes hidden even with underwear or a bikini.

### When can I exercise after surgery?

The return to sports should be progressive and always following the surgeon's instructions. Generally, during the first few weeks, it is recommended to avoid exertion, impact, and intense exercise, especially those involving the upper body. The usual approach is to resume light activity first and leave more demanding sports for a later stage of recovery.

### Can I breastfeed after breast augmentation?

Yes, in most cases. The approach (inframammary or axillary) and the technique used preserve the milk ducts. In the pre-operative consultation, we evaluate your specific case and plan the surgery while preserving lactation function.
