## What is rhinoplasty?

The **Ultrasonic rhinoplasty** is the surgical intervention that modifies the shape, size, and proportions of the nose to harmonize it better with the rest of the face. It can be performed for **aesthetic as well as functional reasons** (for example, to improve breathing).

In our clinic in Barcelona, we address cases of **Aquiline nose, drooping tip, dorsal hump, asymmetries**, and also **Revision rhinoplasty** to correct previous results.

## What You Need to Know Before Your Rhinoplasty: Risks and Preoperative Information

At Clínica Belba, we believe medical transparency is part of care. Like any surgical intervention, rhinoplasty carries risks that are important to know before making a decision — and our plastic surgeons will explain them to you in detail during your pre-operative consultation.

**Most frequent** *(they resolve themselves during the first few months in the vast majority of cases)*

* Swelling and bruising around the nose and eyes in the first few days
* Mild discomfort managed with scheduled pain relief
* Temporary nasal congestion while the inflammation goes down

**Less common**

* Adverse reaction to anesthesia, with possible difficulty breathing
* Infection development
* Bleeding and swelling

Frequently Asked Questions

## Frequently Asked Questions about Rhinoplasty

Solve your most common questions about rhinoplasty in Barcelona

### What is the price of a rhinoplasty in Barcelona?

It is approximately between €3,000 and €10,000, with most interventions between €5,000 and €8,000. The ultrasonic one may have a slightly higher cost due to piezoelectric technology. The first consultation at Clínica Belba is free.

### Rhinoplasty leaves small scars, usually hidden. In an open rhinoplasty, a small scar will be visible on the columella (the strip of skin between the nostrils). In a closed rhinoplasty, all incisions are made inside the nostrils, so there are no visible external scars. In both cases, the scars are usually very fine and fade over time, becoming almost imperceptible.

Closed rhinoplasty has no external incisions and leaves no scar. The open technique leaves a very small mark under the nose, practically imperceptible over time.

### Is nose surgery painful?

It is not painful; however, during the first few days, there may be discomfort, swelling, and bruising, which are managed with prescribed pain relief.

### Is ultrasonic rhinoplasty better than conventional?

Ultrasound offers less bone trauma and usually involves fewer bruises and swelling, but the choice depends on each case and is decided during the assessment.

### Will I be able to breathe better after rhinoplasty?

Yes, when aesthetic and functional correction (septum, turbinates) are combined in a rhinoseptoplasty.

### How long is the recovery?

Most patients resume light social activities within 1–2 weeks. The swelling gradually subsides, and the final result is apparent between 6 and 12 months.

### Rhinoplasty is contraindicated in cases of: \* Unrealistic expectations from the patient. \* Active infection in

It is contraindicated in people with poorly controlled diabetes, obesity, immunosuppression, heart problems, or uncontrolled hypertension. The preoperative assessment is done on a case-by-case basis.

### What happens in the first few days after surgery?

The surgeon will apply a splint for the first few days and will prescribe antibiotics and painkillers for at least a week. The area should not get wet during cleaning.

### There is no limit to how many times a rhinoplasty can be performed.

It can happen again (secondary rhinoplasty), though each reoperation is more complex: that's why choosing the right surgeon the first time is key.

### Is sensation lost after surgery?

There may be a temporary alteration in sensation in the tip of the nose that gradually recovers.
