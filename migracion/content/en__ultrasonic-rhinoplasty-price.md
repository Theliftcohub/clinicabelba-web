## Rhinoplasty Types

The main difference is in how the nasal bone is worked.

* In the **traditional rhinoplasty**, ; some maneuvers are performed with classic instruments to fracture and reshape the bones.

* In the **Ultrasonic rhinoplasty**, high-precision devices are used, which allow for more controlled action on the bone.

The [Rhinomodelation](https://clinicabelba.com/en/rhinomodeling-barcelona/) It is an alternative to traditional rhinoplasty that allows correcting small imperfections without surgery using hyaluronic acid. It is one of the most sought-after options by patients who wish to improve the shape of their nose without going under the knife.

This does not mean that one technique is always better than the other in all cases. The choice depends on nasal anatomy, patient goals, and surgeon judgment.

## Advantages of Ultrasonic Rhinoplasty

Among the most notable advantages of ultrasonic rhinoplasty, the following are often cited:

* Greater precision in bone treatment
* Better surgical control
* Less aggression towards nearby tissues
* Possibility of a more bearable postoperative period in some cases
* More defined results on certain noses

## Possible disadvantages or limitations

Although it is a highly valued technique, it is also worth explaining its limitations. Ultrasonic rhinoplasty:

* It does not substitute the surgeon's judgment
* It does not guarantee a better outcome in all cases by itself.
* It may have a higher cost than other techniques
* It must be indicated in a personalized way

The quality of the outcome largely depends on a good indication, precise planning, and surgical experience.

## When is ultrasonic rhinoplasty worth considering?

Ultrasonic rhinoplasty may be especially worthwhile when seeking:

* Precise remodeling of the bony dorsum
* Correct nasal irregularities
* Tune the nose with maximum precision
* Improve facial harmony with an advanced technique

Not all patients need the exact same technique. Therefore, the recommendation must be made after an individual medical evaluation.

## What is the surgery and recovery like

The intervention is usually performed in an operating room under general anesthesia. During the surgery, the nasal structure is worked on to correct the shape of the nose and, if necessary, also improve its function.

After the surgery, the post-operative period includes:

* Initial inflammation and congestion
* Medical check-ups
* Splint removal based on progression
* Gradual reintegration into routine

The swelling subsides progressively, but the final result of rhinoplasty is evaluated in the medium and long term.

## Frequently Asked Questions about Ultrasonic Rhinoplasty Cost

### How much does ultrasonic rhinoplasty cost?

The price varies depending on the case, the complexity of the surgery, the surgeon, the hospital, and what the treatment includes. The most accurate way to find out is with a personalized evaluation.

### Is ultrasonic rhinoplasty more expensive than traditional rhinoplasty?

In many cases, it can be more expensive, although it depends on the center, the surgeon, and the type of surgery.

### What is normally included in the price?

It usually includes consultation, planning, surgery, anesthesia, operating room, and follow-ups, although it may vary depending on the clinic.

### Is an ultrasonic rhinoplasty worth it?

It can be worthwhile when seeking a precise technique for reshaping the nasal bone, but the indication depends on each patient.

### What is the difference between ultrasonic and traditional rhinoplasty?

The main difference is in the instrument and how the nasal bone is worked. Ultrasound allows for more precise manipulation in certain phases of the surgery.

### Can ultrasonic rhinoplasty be done if there are also breathing problems?

Yes, in some cases it can be combined with functional corrections, although that should be evaluated by the surgeon.

### Does the price change if it's a revision rhinoplasty?

Yes. A revision rhinoplasty is typically more complex and therefore can be more expensive.
