## What is liposuction surgery?

The **liposuction or liposculpture** It is a surgical procedure that removes excess fat by aspirating it with **fine cannulas** inserted through small incisions into the fatty deposits located under the skin.

With the suction of excess fat, you achieve **shape the treated area**, with the consequent improvement of the figure and contour. Each case is evaluated individually.

How to do it: step-by-step procedure

## What you should know before your liposuction: risks and pre-operative information

At Clínica Belba, we believe medical transparency is part of care. Like any surgical procedure, liposuction carries risks that are important to know before making a decision—and our plastic surgeons explain them to you in detail during the pre-operative consultation.

**Most frequent** *(they resolve themselves during the first few months in the vast majority of cases)*

* Mild to moderate discomfort in the first few days, with normal swelling and bruising
* Relative rest 48–72 hours, with gentle walks starting the next day
* Compression garment for 3–6 weeks

**Less common**

* Bleeding or seroma that may require drainage
* Irregularities of the contour or insufficient skin retraction, depending on skin quality
* Scarring alterations, more frequent in smokers
* Clots: prevented with early mobilization

Frequently Asked Questions

## Frequently Asked Questions about Liposuction

Solve your most common doubts about liposculpture in Barcelona

### Is liposuction painful?

Generally, there is moderate discomfort that is managed with medication. Thanks to techniques like Lipo VASER or laser, the postoperative period is smoother.

### What is the price of liposuction in Barcelona?

Between €1,900 and €7,000; most medium-complexity procedures range from €3,000 to €4,500. Advanced techniques, such as VASER liposculpture, start at around €3,500. The first consultation at Clínica Belba is free.

### How much time off work will I need?

It depends on the area and volume treated: for small areas, 2-3 days; for multiple areas, 1-2 weeks.

### What's the difference between conventional liposuction and VASER liposuction?

The conventional method breaks up and manually suctions fat with a cannula; VASER uses ultrasound to extract it more selectively, with better skin retraction and less inflammation.

### What's better, conventional liposuction or VASER liposuction?

Both have advantages: the conventional method is the most established and effective technique for large volumes in one session; VASER causes less pain and swelling and provides more definition. The choice depends on your specific case and is decided during the consultation.

### When are the results seen?

Initial results are apparent within the first days or weeks; final results appear between one and twelve months later, when the skin has finished adapting to the new contour.

### What is a good time for a Lipo 360?

It depends on your goals: some patients prefer winter, with more time to recover; others prefer spring. The important thing is to plan it with your surgeon.

### Who cannot get liposuction?

Overweight and obesity may contraindicate it: it is not a weight loss treatment. The preoperative consultation rules out any health complications.

### What scars remain?

The incisions are minimal (less than 1 cm) and usually become less visible over time.

### Will I regain weight in the treated areas?

The extracted fat cells do not regenerate, but fat can accumulate in untreated areas if an unhealthy lifestyle is maintained.
