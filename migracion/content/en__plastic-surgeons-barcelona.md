## Frequently Asked Questions about Surgeons in Barcelona

### How do I know if a plastic surgeon in Barcelona is properly qualified?

You must verify that they are a specialist in “Plastic, Aesthetic, and Reconstructive Surgery.”.
Furthermore, it is advisable to review their specific experience, how many times they have performed the procedure you are interested in, and to ask to see “before and after” photos of other patients. At Clinica Belba, we have the best breast surgeon in Barcelona, Dr. Dewever!

### ¿Qué criterios debo tener en cuenta al elegir un buen cirujano plástico Barcelona?

You must have accredited training, real experience in the desired intervention, and work in an authorized clinic or center.
It is also important to consider the reputation of plastic surgeons, opinions of other patients, clarity of communication, and transparency about risks and expectations.

Plastic surgeon near me

### Is plastic surgery safe? How risky are these procedures?

As with any surgery, there are risks, which is why it is vital that the center is accredited and the professional is trained.
A good plastic surgeon should explain the possible risks, the type of anesthesia, postoperative care, and how complications are managed. At Clinica Belba, we have the best plastic surgeon in Barcelona.

### ¿Qué resultados puedo esperar y son definitivos tras una cirugía plástica?

It depends on the type of intervention. For example, in the case of a [Breast augmentation BRST technique](https://clinicabelba.com/en/breast-augmentation-brst-technique/) or liposuction, if a healthy lifestyle (diet and exercise) is maintained, the results usually last.
But it's important to understand that no surgery gives “perfect” or “eternal” results: realistic expectations, possible scars, and future bodily changes should be evaluated.

### What questions should I ask during my first consultation with a plastic surgeon?

Some of the most useful ones are:

* I am a large language model, trained by Google.
* How much experience do you have performing this procedure in Spain specifically?
* Where is the operation performed and is the center accredited?
* What realistic results can I expect, and what are the post-operative care/restrictions?
* Do you have previous cases with results and “before and after” that I can review?
