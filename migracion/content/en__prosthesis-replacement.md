## Breast Prosthesis Replacement: When and How It's Done

There is no predetermined time to change implants: they are changed when there is a reason. These are the most common reasons and how we address them.

♡

What is implant revision surgery?

Exceptionally, implants can lose their shape, deteriorate, or rupture. In this surgery, the old prostheses are removed and replaced with new ones. It also serves to increase or decrease volume if you are not satisfied with your breast augmentation: changing to smaller or larger prostheses.

Ideal if your implant has problems or you want to change size

* Extraction + new implant
* Size or shape change
* Final results

✦

When is implant replacement recommended?

Due to implant rupture or displacement, discomfort from capsular contracture, recurrent seromas, sagging after breastfeeding or weight loss, or the desire to change the size, shape, or type of implant. A diagnostic consultation with ultrasound defines your personalized plan.

Diagnostic consultation with breast ultrasound

* Break or displacement
* Capsular contracture
* Size exchange

Capsulectomy

ϟ

Tear, strain, and seromas: what do we do

In an intracapsular rupture, the gel remains within the capsule; in an extracapsular rupture, it leaks into other tissues and can cause inflammation and pain. Capsular contracture hardens and retracts the capsule, and textured implants can generate seromas due to friction. Each case is resolved by removing the implant and, if necessary, the capsule (capsulectomy).

Each cause has its surgical approach

* Intra/extracapsular fracture
* Capsulectomy if applicable
* Recurrent seromas

∿

Technique: same approach route

For the replacement, the same approach is used as in your previous surgery: an incision over the previous scar, removal of the old implant, and placement of the new one, either subglandular or submuscular depending on the case. For capsulotomies or when combined with a lift, the incision may be periareolar or anchor-shaped.

No new scars in single replacement

* Incision in the previous scar
* Plan according to the case
* Combinable with lift

◎

General anesthesia and 90 minutes

This is performed under general anesthesia for your comfort and safety. A simple augmentation can take 90 minutes; combined with a breast lift (mastopexy), it can last up to two hours. You will choose the new implant with your surgeon: round, ergonomic, more discreet, or more voluminous.

Choose the new implant with your surgeon

* General anesthesia
* 90 min (simple replacement)
* Up to 2 hours with elevation

## How to Prepare for Your Prosthetic Replacement: Step by Step

We guide you step-by-step to ensure your breast surgery is completely calm and safe.

In the first consultation, the surgeon performs a physical examination and opens your medical record. After palpating and observing your breasts, they may order a breast ultrasound to check the implant's position and decide on the surgical approach. They will inform you about the risks, duration, and recovery time.

Pre-procedure recommendations: maintain a healthy weight, avoid tobacco and alcohol, fast for 8 hours before surgery, schedule your recovery time, and adjust anticoagulant medication intake as directed by the team.

The procedure is performed under general anesthesia. A simple revision takes about 90 minutes; if combined with mastopexy, it can last up to two hours. Surgical marking and final interview with the anesthesiologist before entering.

## Frequently Asked Questions about Breast Implant Revision

Resolve your doubts about breast implant replacement

## The technique

### What is breast implant revision surgery?

This is the intervention in which old implants are removed and new ones are placed. It corrects problems such as rupture, capsular contracture, or seromas, and also allows you to change the size, shape, or type of implant if you are not satisfied with your previous breast augmentation.

### Is it mandatory to change breast implants?

No. Current implants are made of safe and durable materials, and there is no predetermined time to replace them. They are only replaced if a problem arises (capsular contracture, rupture, sagging) or if you wish to change the size or shape.

### How long do breast implants last?

There is a misconception that they only last 10 years. In reality, quality implants can last 15, 20 years or more, even a lifetime. The important thing is periodic check-ups with ultrasounds to monitor their condition.

## The process

### What is the cost of breast implant revision surgery?

It is usually priced between approximately €3,900 and €7,500, depending on the complexity of the case, whether capsulotomy, reconstruction, or a change in implant plane is required. The estimate usually includes the operating room, medical team fees, anesthesia, and postoperative follow-up.

### What anesthesia is used and how long does the surgery last?

It is performed under general anesthesia for your comfort and safety. A simple exchange can take about 90 minutes; if combined with a mastopexy, it can last up to two hours.

### What is the prior consultation like?

The surgeon will perform a physical examination, open your medical record, and may order a breast ultrasound to assess the prosthesis's position and decide on the surgical approach. They will inform you about the risks, surgical duration, and recovery time.

## Recovery

### What is the post-operative recovery like after a prosthesis replacement?

It's usually more manageable than the first increase: since the implant pocket is already formed, there's hardly any pain during a simple replacement. In the first 7 days, you can resume gentle routines; exercise is contraindicated for up to 4 weeks.

### What precautions should be taken?

Rest, support bra for the duration indicated by the surgeon, scar hygiene, antibiotic medication, and do not lift your arms above your shoulders for the first few days.

### When is a good time for implant replacement?

Cooler seasons like autumn and winter favor healing, although you'll schedule the perfect timing according to your calendar and the surgeon's evaluation.

## Results and scars

### What scars are left by prosthesis replacement?

In a simple revision, the scar is the same as your previous breast augmentation: no new incisions are made. In complex revisions with capsulectomy, there may be a scar around the areola or an inverted T scar under the breast fold, which diminishes over time until it becomes almost imperceptible.

### Is sensation lost after surgery?

Not all patients notice it. If it appears, it is a temporary condition that disappears as the inflammation subsides.

### Can I combine a revision with a breast lift?

Yes. If there is sagging after breastfeeding or weight loss, the implant exchange can be combined with a mastopexy in the same procedure to remove excess tissue and lift the implants.
