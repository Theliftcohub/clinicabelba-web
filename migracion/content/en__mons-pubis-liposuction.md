## Mount Venus

The mons pubis is a prominence of fatty tissue located above the labia majora and normally covered with pubic hair. It is part of the external female genitalia and acts as a protective area for the vulva. Its presence is completely normal; however, excess fat in this area can become visible and cause aesthetic discomfort, especially with tight clothing, bikinis, or sportswear. In some cases, fat accumulation in the mons pubis can also occur in men, although it is more common in women.

## What is mons pubis liposuction?

Mons pubis liposuction is a female intimate surgery that allows for the removal of excess fat localized in the upper pubic area. Through small incisions, the surgeon inserts a suction cannula that removes accumulated adipose tissue, improving the volume of the mons pubis, smoothing the bulge, and achieving an area that is more in proportion with the abdomen and the body's silhouette.

Many women with a prominent or bulging mons pubis opt for this procedure because, despite maintaining healthy habits, they are unable to reduce this volume naturally. The result is a more harmonious pubic area that is discreet under clothing and consistent with the patient's body proportions.

## Mount of Venus, how to reduce it?

Reduction of the mons pubis can be addressed with different alternatives depending on each patient's needs:

**Surgical options:**

* [Liposuction](https://clinicabelba.com/en/liposuction-barcelona/) Conventional: fat extraction using an aspiration cannula.
* [Vaser liposuction](https://clinicabelba.com/en/vaser-liposuction/) Laser-assisted or with BodyTite: melts fat and simultaneously tightens skin and stimulates collagen.
* [Lifting](https://clinicabelba.com/en/facelift-barcelona/) pubic: when there is skin laxity in addition to excess fat.
* Combined treatment: liposuction + lift for more complete results.

**Non-invasive options (more progressive and limited results):**

* Cryolipolysis: reduces adipose tissue through controlled cold.
* Enzyme mesotherapy: injections to dissolve localized fat.

As a supplement, a balanced diet and cardiovascular and strength training help optimize the overall appearance of the area. However, when it comes to stubborn localized fat, mons pubis liposuction offers the most predictable and lasting results.

## Mount of Venus liposuction procedure

### Surgical technique

Mons pubis liposuction is a short, outpatient surgery performed under local anesthesia or sedation. The surgeon makes three small incisions in the area, inserts a suction cannula, and removes accumulated fat, precisely reshaping the pubic mound to fit each patient's body proportions.

In cases where laser-assisted technology (such as BodyTite) is applied, adipose tissue is eliminated using heat. This method offers the added advantage of simultaneously tightening the epidermis, stimulating subcutaneous collagen, and cauterizing blood vessels to minimize bleeding and enhance skin firmness with more lasting results.

When tissues descend, the surgeon can combine liposuction with a pubic lift to tighten the skin, elevate the area, and improve the overall contour.

### Preoperative

The preoperative process begins with an initial consultation in which the surgeon assesses the characteristics of the area—skin texture, amount of accumulated fat, and the patient's general condition—and develops a personalized plan adapted to each case.

During this first visit, necessary laboratory tests and complementary assessments (cardiology, anesthesiology) will be requested. Once the surgery date is confirmed, the following instructions will be provided:

* Completely shave the suprapubic area before surgery
* Inform the surgeon of any ongoing anti-inflammatory medication.
* Stop smoking and drinking alcohol 4 weeks before the procedure and 4 weeks after.
* Clean the area with antiseptic soap the day of surgery

Wear comfortable and light clothing on the day of the procedure.

### Postoperative care and recovery

After liposuction of the mons pubis, the area will present mild swelling and redness during the first few days, which will gradually subside. The use of a compression garment is essential during this phase to promote skin retraction and reduce inflammation.

Bruising and slight discomfort in the labia minora may appear, which is completely normal and usually resolves within 10 days. Approximate recovery times are:

* Activity without physical exertion: within 24-48 hours
* Sexual intercourse: from one week
* Progressive exercise: starting at 3 weeks
* Definitive results: visible as the inflammation subsides

To ensure optimal recovery, the patient must maintain rigorous hygiene of the area, adhere to the prescribed rest, and avoid sudden movements that could affect the incisions.

## Frequently Asked Questions about Mons Pubis Liposuction

### How much does liposuction of the mons pubis cost in Barcelona?

The price of liposuction of the mons pubis in Barcelona typically ranges from 1,200 to 2,500 euros, depending on the technique used, the surgeon's experience, and the complexity of the case. At Clinica Belba, the cost includes the pre-surgical assessment, the procedure in the hospital setting of Grupo Teknon, anesthesia, and complete post-operative follow-up.

### How long is the recovery period?

Recovery is rapid. Most patients resume non-strenuous work activity within 24-48 hours. Inflammation reduces progressively in the first 2 weeks, and exercise can be gradually resumed starting at 3 weeks.

### Does liposuction of the mons pubis leave visible scars?

The incisions are very small (3-5 mm) and are made in discreet areas. Over time, they become practically imperceptible. Proper postoperative care—sun protection and healing creams—accelerates their disappearance.

### Are the results of reducing the mons pubis permanent?

Yes. The removed fat cells do not regenerate. The results are long-lasting as long as the patient maintains a stable weight. Sudden weight changes or new pregnancies can alter the volume of the area over time.

### Can it be combined with other surgeries in the same session?

Yes. Liposuction of the mons pubis is frequently combined with a pubic lift when there is skin laxity. It is also possible to perform it along with other female body or intimate surgeries in the same session, as long as the medical assessment deems it appropriate.

### Is pubis surgery painful?

The surgery is performed under local anesthesia or sedation, so there is no pain during the procedure. In the postoperative period, mild discomfort and swelling may appear, which are easily controlled with oral analgesia during the first few days.

### Can a prominent mons pubis be treated without surgery?

When it comes to stubborn localized fat, non-invasive treatments like cryolipolysis or enzymatic mesotherapy can progressively reduce volume, although their results are more limited. Liposuction of the mons pubis is the most effective option when fat does not respond to healthy habits.

### Who is NOT a candidate for this surgery?

Not recommended for pregnant or breastfeeding women, significantly overweight patients, people with conditions affecting clotting or healing, or those planning a pregnancy in the short term. Prior medical evaluation will determine if you are a suitable candidate.
