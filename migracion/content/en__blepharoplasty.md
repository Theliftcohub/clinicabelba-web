## ¿Qué es la blefaroplastia? Cirugía de párpados caídos y bolsas en los ojos

The **Blepharoplasty** es una cirugía que elimina el exceso de piel, grasa y tejido en los párpados superiores e inferiores. Corrige la apariencia de **ojos caídos o cansados** y elimina las **bolsas bajo los ojos**.

Además de su función estética, tiene beneficios funcionales: cuando el párpado superior cae sobre el campo visual, la blefaroplastia puede **mejorar la visión** del paciente.

## ¿Cuándo está indicada la operación de párpados caídos?

Indicated in patients with **stable weight**, **Good general health** y **Realistic expectations** — women and men who:

✓Presentan exceso de piel en los párpados

✓Tienen bolsas bajo los ojos o apariencia de cansancio persistente

✓No mejoran con tratamientos no quirúrgicos

✓El párpado superior cae sobre el campo visual (indicación funcional)

✓Cualquier adulto en buen estado de salud general

✓They maintain healthy lifestyles

Warning**Important:** It is not a weight loss method. It is not recommended if future pregnancies or significant short-term weight changes are planned. The surgeon evaluates each case individually during the first consultation.

## Lo que debes saber antes de tu blefaroplastia: riesgos e información preoperatoria

En Clínica Belba creemos que la transparencia médica es parte del cuidado. Como cualquier intervención quirúrgica, la blefaroplastia conlleva riesgos que es importante conocer antes de tomar una decisión — y nuestros cirujanos plásticos te los explican con detalle durante la consulta preoperatoria.

**Most frequent** *(they resolve themselves during the first few months in the vast majority of cases)*

* Inflamación, hematomas y molestias leves los primeros días, controladas con analgésicos
* Síntomas temporales: visión borrosa, sensibilidad a la luz u ojos llorosos
* Irritación en la zona de las incisiones

**Less common**

* Infección o sangrado
* Cicatrización visible o cambios temporales en la sensibilidad de la piel
* En casos poco frecuentes, lesiones oculares o visión borrosa transitoria

Frequently Asked Questions

## Preguntas frecuentes sobre la blefaroplastia en Barcelona

Resuelve tus dudas más comunes sobre la cirugía de párpados

### How much does eyelid surgery cost in Barcelona?

The upper portion ranges from €1,300 to €1,995; the lower portion from €1,700 to €2,500; and the complete portion (upper and lower) from €2,300 to €4,890. The exact budget is determined after a personalized medical evaluation, with no cost or obligation.

### The effects of blepharoplasty are generally long-lasting.

The results are long-lasting. Although aging continues its natural course, in most cases a second intervention is not necessary: the effect can be maintained for many years with good skincare habits.

### Can blepharoplasty be done without surgery?

Treatments like fractional laser or radiofrequency can slightly improve eyelid skin quality, but they do not effectively correct excess skin or bags. For a real and lasting result, surgery is the indicated option.

### What anesthesia is used and how long does it last?

With local anesthesia and sedation or general anesthesia, depending on patient preference and complexity. It lasts between 1 and 2 hours and is outpatient: you go home the same day.

### How is the recovery?

In the first few days, there will be swelling, bruising, and mild discomfort, controlled with pain relievers. Temporary symptoms like blurred vision or light sensitivity may appear. Complete healing can be expected in about 15 days.

### What care should I follow after surgery?

Apply cold compresses to the eyes, sleep with your head elevated for the first 3-4 days, and follow the medical team's guidelines. The incision marks become practically unnoticeable over the months.

### Blepharoplasty surgery carries risks, as does any surgical procedure. These can include: \* \*\*Infection:\*\* Like any surgery, there's a risk of infection at the incision sites. \* \*\*Bleeding:\*\* Excessive bleeding can occur during or after surgery. \* \*\*Scarring:\*\* While plastic surgeons strive to make scars as inconspicuous as possible, some scarring is inevitable. The appearance of scars can vary. \* \*\*Dry eyes:\*\* Some patients may experience temporary or persistent dry eyes after surgery. \* \*\*Ectropion:\*\* This is a condition where the lower eyelid droops or turns outward. It can occur if too much skin is removed from the lower eyelid. \* \*\*Entropion:\*\* This is a condition where the eyelid turns inward, causing the eyelashes to rub against the eye. \* \*\*Vision changes:\*\* Although rare, temporary blurred or double vision can occur. Permanent vision loss is extremely rare. \* \*\*Asymmetry:\*\* Uneven results between the two eyes can happen, requiring revision surgery. \* \*\*Anesthesia risks:\*\* There are inherent risks associated with any type of anesthesia. \* \*\*Pain and swelling:\*\* Post-operative pain, swelling, bruising, and discomfort are expected but can sometimes be more significant. \* \*\*Numbness or altered sensation:\*\* You may experience temporary or permanent numbness or changes in sensation around the eyelids. \* \*\*Difficulty closing eyes:\*\* In some cases, it might be difficult to close your eyes completely, which can lead to dryness and irritation. \* \*\*Ptosis (drooping eyelid) recurrence:\*\* If the eyelid muscles were also addressed, there's a small chance the drooping could return over time. It's crucial to discuss all potential risks and complications with your surgeon before undergoing blepharoplasty. They will assess your individual health status and explain which risks are most relevant to you.

The most frequent are infection, bleeding, irritation, visible scarring, or temporary changes in sensation. In rare cases, eye injuries or transient blurred vision. A good postoperative protocol significantly minimizes them.

### Who is a good candidate?

Any healthy adult with excess eyelid skin, under-eye bags, or a tired appearance that doesn't improve with non-surgical treatments; also when the eyelid droops over the field of vision.
