## Rib remodeling, rib dislocation, and floating rib surgery: differences

Although many people use these terms interchangeably, **rib remodeling** refers to the aesthetic procedure aimed at modifying the shape of the waist by acting on the floating ribs. The term **Rib dislocation** is frequently used colloquially, while “floating rib surgery” more generally describes this type of procedure.

## Rib reshaping techniques

Rib reshaping surgery can be performed using different techniques, the most common being traditional techniques and the innovative method. [RibXCar](https://clinicabelba.com/ribxcar-vs-tecnica-tradicional-cual-es-mejor/).

### Traditional Techniques

1. General anesthesia: The patient is sedated to prevent any pain during surgery.
2. Incision: A small incision is made in the lateral or posterior area of the torso, depending on the required access.
3. Removal or remodeling: The surgeon accesses the [floating ribs](https://clinicabelba.com/quitarse-costillas-flotantes-mito-o-realidad/) and performs partial or complete extraction, depending on the aesthetic objective.

### RibXCar Technique

The RibXCar technique is a more modern and advanced technique that uses cutting-edge technology for less invasive rib remodeling.

1. General anesthesia: Same as traditional techniques.
2. Smaller incision: A smaller incision is made to minimize scarring and speed up recovery.
3. Use of advanced technology: The method employs specialized tools to sculpt and reshape the ribs with precision, without needing to remove them completely.
4. Custom modeling: The surgeon can precisely adjust the shape and position of the ribs according to the patient's aesthetic desires.
5. Faster closure and recovery: The smaller incision and less invasive technique allow for a faster recovery with less discomfort.

## Risks of Rib Reshaping Surgery

Rib dislocation surgery can offer surprising aesthetic results, but it's important to know its risks before deciding on this procedure. Like any surgery, it involves certain risks that are crucial to consider:

1. **Postoperative infection** Although it is uncommon, it can occur if medical instructions are not followed properly.
2. **Bleeding during or after the procedure:** May require additional medical attention in severe cases.
3. **Pain and discomfort** Pain in the treated area is normal, but it can be managed with prescribed medication.
4. **Visible scars** Although surgeons aim to minimize scarring, there is a risk of noticeable scars or keloids.
5. **Unsatisfactory or asymmetrical results:** The surgery may not exactly meet expectations, which could require adjustments.
6. **Internal organ damage** Although rare, there is a potential risk of affecting organs such as the lungs.
7. **Respiratory problems** Costal modification can temporarily or permanently affect breathing.
8. **Adverse reactions to anesthesia:** Some patients may experience side effects.
9. **Prolonged recovery:** Recovery can last several weeks, limiting physical activities.
10. **Deep vein thrombosis (DVT)** In rare cases, a blood clot may form, requiring immediate attention.

## Frequently Asked Questions About Rib Remodeling

### What exactly is rib remodeling and how does it differ from a rib dislocation?

La remodelación costal es una cirugía plástica que modifica la forma de las costillas flotantes (10.ª, 11.ª y 12.ª) para afinar la cintura. El término «luxación de costillas» se utiliza popularmente para referirse al mismo procedimiento, aunque técnicamente no implica una luxación real sino una remodelación controlada de la estructura costal. En Clínica Belba diferenciamos además la técnica tradicional del método propio RibXCar, más preciso y menos invasivo.

### How long does a rib reshaping surgery take?

The surgery lasts approximately 1 hour and is performed on an outpatient basis, meaning the patient can return home the same day. No hospital stay is required.

### Can floating ribs be removed without surgery?

No. The only way to modify the structure of the floating ribs is through surgery. Techniques like liposuction can improve waist contour, but they do not affect the bone. If you are looking for a real change in your silhouette at the bone level, rib reshaping is the only option available.

### What's the difference between the traditional technique and RibXCar?

Rib reshaping is a very specific surgery that requires expertise in advanced body surgery. At Clínica Belba, we are leaders in Spain for this procedure: we work with the RibXCar technique and evaluate each case individually to determine if the intervention is appropriate, which technique offers the best outcome, and how to integrate it into a safe surgical plan.

### How long does recovery from rib reshaping take?

Recovery is quick: within 2-3 days, the patient can lead a practically normal life. For intense physical activity, such as going to the gym, 3 months are needed. During that time, it is mandatory to wear a brace all day, as it is what finishes molding the ribs into the desired shape.

### Does rib dislocation recovery hurt?

There is practically no pain. Unlike other body surgeries, rib reshaping causes minimal discomfort in the postoperative period, which is easily managed with prescribed medication. The greatest discomfort is usually the continued use of the corset during the first 3 months.

### What scar does surgery on the floating ribs leave?

The scar is very small, approximately 1 cm, and is located on a lateral area of the torso that remains hidden with clothing. This is one of the most notable advantages compared to other body contouring surgeries. With proper care, the scar gradually lightens in the following months.

### How much does costal remodeling cost in Spain?

The cost of rib reconstruction in Spain ranges from €6,000 to €12,000, depending on the technique used, the team's experience, and the clinic's facilities. At Clínica Belba, the exact quote is determined after an individual medical assessment, with no cost or obligation.

### Who is not a candidate for rib reshaping?

Costal remodeling is not suitable for patients who are significantly overweight, as liposuction is usually the first recommended step in those cases. It is also not indicated for people with previous respiratory problems, active bone diseases, or unrealistic expectations about the outcome. The indication is always confirmed during a personalized medical evaluation.

### Can rib restructuring be combined with other surgeries?

Yes. Rib reshaping is compatible with breast augmentation, liposuction, and blepharoplasties, among others. It is not recommended to combine it with surgeries that require skin removal, such as abdominoplasty. The possibility of combining procedures is always evaluated individually during the prior consultation.
