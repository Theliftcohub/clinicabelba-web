## Nose reshaping, also known as liquid rhinoplasty or a non-surgical nose job, is a cosmetic procedure that uses injectable dermal fillers to temporarily alter the shape of the nose. It can correct: \* \*\*Nasal asymmetry:\*\* Improve the balance and symmetry of the nose. \* \*\*Dorsal humps (or bumps):\*\* Smooth out a prominent bump on the bridge of the nose. \* \*\*A depressed nasal bridge:\*\* Add volume to create a straighter or more defined bridge. \* \*\*A drooping nasal tip:\*\* Lift the tip of the nose slightly to create a more refined profile. \* \*\*Mild irregularities and imperfections:\*\* Address minor dents or asymmetries. It's important to note that nose reshaping is a temporary solution and the results typically last from several months to a couple of years, depending on the filler used and individual metabolism. It cannot change the size of the nose dramatically or address functional breathing problems.

The **Rhinomodelation** It is a medical treatment. **non-surgical** of nasal beautification and harmony based on **Resorbable fillers**. It works especially well for correcting the bridge, smoothing a small hump, lifting the tip, correcting asymmetries, and refining previous rhinoplasties.

it doesn't reduce a large nose or correct complex functional problems: for those cases, a **rhinoplasty** It could be the right option.

## Rhinomodeling is indicated for people who want to correct aesthetic imperfections of the nose without surgery, such as: \* Slightly crooked nose \* Dorsal hump \* Dropped tip \* Asymmetric nostrils \* Small deficits or depressions in the nose \* To improve the balance and harmony of the face It is important to note that this procedure is not intended for significant structural changes or breathing problems, for which plastic surgery would be recommended. A prior consultation with a qualified professional is essential to determine if rhinomodeling is the appropriate treatment.

Indicated in patients with **stable weight**, **Good general health** y **Realistic expectations** — women and men who:

✓They are looking for subtle profile corrections: aligning the bridge of the nose or smoothing a small hump.

✓They want to define or lift the tip, or harmonize angles

✓They have mild asymmetries or irregularities, even after previous rhinoplasty.

✓They have healthy skin, with no infection or active dermatitis in the area.

✓Realistic expectations: improving contour, not reducing nose size

✓They maintain healthy lifestyles

Warning**Important:** It is not a weight loss method. It is not recommended if future pregnancies or significant short-term weight changes are planned. The surgeon evaluates each case individually during the first consultation.

## What you should know before your rhinoplasty: safety and contraindications

At Clínica Belba, we believe that medical transparency is part of care. Like any surgical intervention, rhinoplasty carries risks that are important to know before making a decision—and our plastic surgeons explain them to you in detail during the preoperative consultation.

**Most frequent** *(they resolve themselves during the first few months in the vast majority of cases)*

* Mild pressure and “prickling” during the session
* Possible subtle swelling in the first 48–72 hours
* Mild and temporary discomfort

**Less common**

* If you need to reduce size or correct function (severely deviated septum, valve): it's better to consider rhinoplasty
* Large gibbous or pronounced structural deformities
* Pregnancy or breastfeeding, active infections, or uncontrolled bleeding disorders

Frequently Asked Questions

## Frequently Asked Questions about Rhinoplasty

Solve your most common questions about nose sculpting

### Does rhinoplasty hurt?

With anesthetic cream and a measured technique, it is very well tolerated: mild and temporary discomfort.

### How long does nose modeling take?

12 to 18 months on average. For post-rhinoplasty touch-ups and stable dorsums, sometimes more; for very mobile tips, slightly less.

### Can I live a normal life afterward?

Yes. Avoid gyms, heavy glasses, and strong heat for 24-48 hours. Light makeup after that time.

### How much does a non-surgical rhinoplasty cost?

Between €300 and €600 per session as a guideline (average: €400–€450 per vial). It depends on the complexity and the product: personalized quote after consultation assessment.

### Can it be combined with botulinum toxin?

Yes, especially for lifting a tip that drops due to the septum depressor. It usually adds naturalness to the result.

### Is rhinoplasty reversible?

Hyaluronic acid is absorbable and, if necessary, hyaluronidase can be used to adjust it.
