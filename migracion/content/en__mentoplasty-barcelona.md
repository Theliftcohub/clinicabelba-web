## ¿Qué es la mentoplastia? Cirugía plástica del mentón

The **Mentoplasty** es una cirugía plástica que corrige cómo se ve y se proyecta el mentón. Puede realizarse con **implant** (add volume) or via **sliding osteotomy** (move your own bone, even adjust height and small asymmetries).

The goal isn't just to make the chin bigger or smaller: the aim is **proportion with nose, lips, and jaw**. A balanced chin favors lip seal and defines the cervicofacial angle.

Treatments

Mentoplasty techniques: implant or sliding osteotomy?

There are two main routes and **Both can be very natural if indicated well.**. In most cases the incision is **intraoral**, so there's no visible scar.

01

Chin implant

Add volume for a mild to moderate projection increase with favorable tissues. Routine intraoral incision, quick and reversible recovery: can be removed.

[Request appraisal](#formulario)

02

Sliding osteotomy

Move your own bone forward or backward, adjusting height and minor asymmetries with millimeter precision. It is secured with a plate and internal screws that are neither seen nor felt.

[Request appraisal](#formulario)

| Naturalidad al tacto | Excelente si bien indicado | Excelente (es tu hueso) |

## ¿Para quién está indicada la cirugía de mentón?

Indicated in patients with **stable weight**, **Good general health** y **Realistic expectations** — women and men who:

✓Tienen el mentón retraído o corto (microgenia), con falta de soporte al labio inferior

✓Tienen un mentón muy salido que domina el perfil y endurece la expresión

✓Presentan asimetrías leves-moderadas que rompen la armonía del perfil

✓El cuello parece más ancho de lo que es por falta de soporte anterior

✓Gozan de buena salud y tienen expectativas realistas (si el problema es de mordida, se valora ortognática)

✓They maintain healthy lifestyles

Warning**Important:** It is not a weight loss method. It is not recommended if future pregnancies or significant short-term weight changes are planned. The surgeon evaluates each case individually during the first consultation.

## Lo que debes saber antes de tu mentoplastia: riesgos y cómo los minimizamos

En Clínica Belba creemos que la transparencia médica es parte del cuidado. Como cualquier intervención quirúrgica, la mentoplastia conlleva riesgos que es importante conocer antes de tomar una decisión — y nuestros cirujanos plásticos te los explican con detalle durante la consulta preoperatoria.

**Most frequent** *(they resolve themselves during the first few months in the vast majority of cases)*

* Molestia leve-moderada los primeros días, controlable con analgesia
* Edema esperable con pico a las 48–72 h, baja en la primera semana
* Sensación de tirantez y rigidez inicial que desaparece en 1–3 meses

**Less common**

* Hematoma o infección
* Alteraciones temporales de la sensibilidad (nervio mentoniano): suelen remitir en semanas-meses
* Asimetría o necesidad de retoques, poco frecuentes con una indicación correcta

Frequently Asked Questions

## Preguntas frecuentes sobre la mentoplastia

Resuelve tus dudas más comunes sobre la cirugía de mentón

### Who is a good candidate for mentoplasty?

Individuals with a recessive chin, excessive chin, or asymmetries who seek facial balance and are in good health. If the problem is with the jaw or maxilla globally, orthognathic surgery is considered.

### Implant or osteotomy: what's best for me?

Implant for mild-moderate projection increase with favorable tissues; osteotomy when precision is needed for projection, height, or asymmetries. The choice depends on your anatomy and goals.

### Local or general anesthesia?

The usual is local anesthesia + sedation and discharge the same day. For complex combined procedures, general anesthesia.

### How long does the surgery take?

Approximately 20–60 minutes, depending on technique and case.

### Does a mentoplasty hurt?

Mild-moderate discomfort controllable with painkillers; initial tightness is normal.

### Mentoplasty does not correct bite or apnea.

No. If it affects occlusion or the airway due to skeletal issues, orthognathic surgery is considered: mentoplasty refines the chin, it does not correct the bite.

### Are the results permanent?

Yes, both with implants and osteotomy; the final outcome settles in months, when the swelling subsides.

### When can I return to work and sports?

Office work in 5–7 days; progressive sport from 3–4 weeks.
