## What is the price of gynecomastia surgery?

The [**the cost of gynecomastia**](https://clinicabelba.com/en/gynecomastia-cost/) It's not usually fixed because it depends on the type of male breast tissue to be treated, the volume of tissue, whether there's glandular tissue or just fat, and the degree of correction needed.

A mild gynecomastia treated with liposuction does not cost the same as a more pronounced gynecomastia that requires gland excision, contour remodeling, and more extensive surgery. Therefore, the final cost must be tailored to each patient.

### Indicative price ranges for gynecomastia surgery in Spain

In Spain, the **Cost of gynecomastia surgery** is usually placed illustratively between **€4,000 and €7,500**, although the final cost may vary depending on the degree of gynecomastia, the technique used, and the services included in the treatment.

Roughly, the most common ranges can be:

* **Mild gynecomastia treated primarily with liposuction**between **€4,000 and €5,000**
* **Gynecomastia with a glandular component requiring more extensive surgery**between **€5,000 and €6,500**
* **More severe gynecomastia or more complex cases**between **€6,500 and €7,500 or more**

These prices are indicative and may change depending on the city, clinic, surgeon, hospital, and follow-up included in the estimate.

## What is gynecomastia and when is surgery performed?

Gynecomastia is the enlargement of the male breast. It can be due to the growth of mammary glands, localized excess fat, or a combination of both factors.

Surgery is usually recommended when:

* The [breast augmentation](https://clinicabelba.com/en/aumento-de-mama-hibrido/) It is persistent
* It doesn't improve with exercise or weight loss
* Is there aesthetic or emotional discomfort?
* There is marked asymmetry
* The patient wants a flatter, more masculine chest.

### Types of Gynecomastia: Why They Affect the Price

Gynecomastia does not always present in the same way. There are mild, moderate, and more advanced cases, and this directly influences the treatment and the price.

In general:

* **Mild degrees** tend to require less correction
* **Moderate degrees** may need [liposuction](https://clinicabelba.com/en/liposuction-barcelona/) glandular resection
* **High degrees** may involve more remodeling and sometimes excess skin

The more advanced the grade, the more complex the surgery can be, and the higher the final cost can be.

## Difference between gynecomastia and pseudogynecomastia

It's an important difference, because it also changes the treatment.

### Gynecomastia

* Is there actual growth of male breast tissue?.

### Pseudogynecomastia

* Localized fat predominates, with no significant glandular enlargement.

This distinction can only be correctly assessed through medical examination, and it is key to knowing which surgical technique each patient needs.

### Does Social Security cover gynecomastia?

In most cases, gynecomastia is not covered by social security when the indication is primarily aesthetic. It is only in very specific situations that it may be considered within the public system, always under specific medical criteria.

## The recovery from gynecomastia surgery is generally straightforward and involves several stages: \*\*Immediately After Surgery (First 24-48 Hours):\*\* \* \*\*Discomfort and Swelling:\*\* You will likely experience some pain, swelling, bruising, and tenderness in the chest area. This is normal and can be managed with prescribed pain medication. \* \*\*Compression Garment:\*\* You will need to wear a special compression vest or garment almost continuously for several weeks. This helps to reduce swelling, support the chest tissues, and promote even healing. \* \*\*Activity Restrictions:\*\* You'll be advised to rest and avoid strenuous activities, heavy lifting, and any movements that put stress on the chest muscles. Light walking is often encouraged to help with circulation and prevent blood clots. \* \*\*Drainage Tubes (Sometimes):\*\* In some cases, small drainage tubes may be placed under the skin to remove excess fluid. These are typically removed within a day or two. \*\*First Week:\*\* \* \*\*Soreness and Swelling:\*\* Discomfort and swelling will begin to subside but will still be present. You may notice some numbness or altered sensation in the chest area, which usually improves over time. \* \*\*Wound Care:\*\* You'll receive instructions on how to care for your incision sites, which may involve keeping them clean and dry. \* \*\*Gradual Return to Light Activities:\*\* You can usually resume light daily activities, but heavy exercise and contact sports are still off-limits. \* \*\*Follow-up Appointment:\*\* You'll likely have a follow-up appointment with your surgeon to check on your progress and remove any stitches that are not dissolvable. \*\*Weeks 2-6:\*\* \* \*\*Significant Swelling Reduction:\*\* Most of the significant swelling will have gone down, though some residual swelling may persist for several months. \* \*\*Increased Activity:\*\* You can gradually increase your activity level, including returning to desk work and light exercise. \* \*\*Continued Compression:\*\* You'll likely continue wearing the compression garment, though your surgeon may advise reducing the duration of wear. \* \*\*Improved Sensation:\*\* Sensation in the chest area should continue to improve. \*\*Long-Term Recovery (Months 1-6 and Beyond):\*\* \* \*\*Final Results:\*\* The final results of the surgery will become more apparent as all swelling fully resolves. This can take up to six months or longer. \* \*\*Scar Maturation:\*\* Incision scars will continue to fade and mature, becoming less noticeable over time. \* \*\*Return to Full Activity:\*\* You can typically resume all normal physical activities, including vigorous exercise and contact sports, as cleared by your surgeon. \* \*\*Maintaining Results:\*\* Maintaining a healthy weight and lifestyle is important to prevent the recurrence of gynecomastia. \*\*Factors Affecting Recovery:\*\* \* \*\*Extent of Gynecomastia:\*\* More extensive cases may require a longer recovery. \* \*\*Surgical Technique:\*\* Different techniques (e.g., liposuction alone, gland excision, or a combination) can influence recovery duration. \* \*\*Individual Healing:\*\* Everyone heals at a different pace. \* \*\*Adherence to Post-Operative Instructions:\*\* Following your surgeon's advice is crucial for optimal results and a smooth recovery. It's essential to have a thorough discussion with your plastic surgeon before the operation about what to expect during your individual recovery process, including potential risks and complications. They will provide you with specific post-operative instructions tailored to your needs.

## Gynaecomastia Pricing FAQ

### How much does gynecomastia surgery cost?

The price depends on the grade, surgical technique, whether it's combined with liposuction, and the included services. The best way to find out is with a personalized consultation.

### What is the price of gynecomastia surgery in Barcelona?

In Barcelona, the indicative range is usually between **€4,500 and €7,500**, although it may vary depending on the case.

### What is included in the price of gynecomastia surgery?

It usually includes consultation, planning, surgery, anesthesia, operating room, and follow-up appointments, although it depends on each clinic.

### Does Social Security cover gynecomastia?

Only in very specific cases and under specific medical criteria. When the indication is aesthetic, it is usually not covered.

### Is gynecomastia with glandular tissue more expensive than one with only fat?

Yes, in many cases it can be more complex because it requires a more extensive surgery than a simple liposuction.

### What is the difference between gynecomastia and pseudogynecomastia?

Gynecomastia involves glandular tissue; pseudogynecomastia is primarily localized fat.

### How long is the recovery?

Initial recovery is usually from a few days to a few weeks, depending on the type of surgery and the patient's progress.

### Does the price change if the gynecomastia is severe?

Yes. Generally, the higher the degree and the more complex the correction, the higher the price can be.
