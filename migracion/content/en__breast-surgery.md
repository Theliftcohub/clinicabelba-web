# Breast surgery in Barcelona

Since ancient times, breasts have been associated with feminine beauty, fertility, and sensuality. And for today's standard of charm and seduction, it's almost indispensable for a woman to have proportionate and firm breasts. Not for nothing, the **breast surgery** It is one of the most in-demand cosmetic procedures in many countries around the world.

While there is no ideal shape or size for female breasts, it's common to find women who are unhappy with their breasts. If this is your case, you can opt for both non-surgical treatments and **cirugía plástica de mamas**, to get the desired result. And over here in the **Clínica Belba** We specialize in advanced surgical and aesthetic medicine, so we can help you get the breasts you've dreamed of.

![Breast surgery](https://clinicabelba.com/wp-content/uploads/elementor/thumbs/Cirugia-de-pecho-rkeie2r3m50fgqf9pt6ia4kaccoe79x3m5q6v9vdeo.jpg?wsr "Breast surgery")

## **Why choose breast surgery?**

Generally, the **breast surgery** is associated with the **cosmetic surgery** that is carried out [breast augmentation](https://clinicabelba.com/en/breast-augmentation-barcelona/) through the use of implants. However, there are other **cirugías plásticas de pecho**, which are aimed at reconfiguring them as an increase of [Chest preservation technique](https://clinicabelba.com/en/breast-augmentation-preserve/) or el [Breast augmentation BRST technique](https://clinicabelba.com/en/breast-augmentation-brst-technique/), and even to reduce them, like the [breast reduction](https://clinicabelba.com/en/breast-reduction/). The goal of all these surgeries is the same: to shape the breasts so they look in accordance with the taste and needs of the woman undergoing the procedure.

In general, the main reasons for breast aesthetic surgery are as follows:

* **Breast asymmetry** In other words, when one breast is larger than the other, or both breasts have noticeably different shapes.
* **Voluptuous breasts** Large breasts, in comparison to the rest of the body, often cause health problems, such as back pain and breast dermatitis.
* **Small or tubular breasts:** This operation of [breast augmentation in Barcelona](https://clinicabelba.com/en/breast-augmentation-barcelona/) it is purely aesthetic, and is performed to give greater volume and harmonious shape to the breasts.
* **Breast reconstruction:** In some high-risk breast cancer patients, there is no other option but to remove the breast. And to restore the aesthetic appearance of the breast, an implant is placed.
* **Sagging breasts** The passage of time and pregnancies cause the natural sagging of breasts, and with various **Aesthetic treatments**, as in [chest lift in Barcelona](https://clinicabelba.com/en/breast-lift/) these can be returned to their starting position.
* **Areola reduction** Entre las cirugías plásticas más solicitadas se encuentra la [areola reduction](https://clinicabelba.com/en/areola-reduction/). But patients aren't only concerned about size; they're also concerned about asymmetries and **large areolas**.

## **Can I breastfeed after breast surgery?**

Muchas mujeres se preocupan por la posibilidad de amamantar luego de someterse a una cirugía plástica de mamas. Depende del tipo de intervención y del procedimiento empleado que luego de la misma se pueda o no amamantar sin problema alguno. Por ello, en **Clínica Belba** we invite you to consult with **our expert surgeons** on this important topic.

## **Do augmented breasts look natural?**

The answer is a resounding yes, as long as the size of the breasts is in proportion with the rest of the body.
