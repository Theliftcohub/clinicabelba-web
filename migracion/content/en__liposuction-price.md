## What is the price of liposuction?

The **Price of liposuction** depends on several factors: treating a small area like the [Mons pubis liposuction](https://clinicabelba.com/en/mons-pubis-liposuction/) that you want to undergo a more extensive body remodeling. It's also not the same as an abdomen-focused surgery like [HD Liposuction](https://clinicabelba.com/en/high-definition-liposuction/) that an intervention combining abdomen, flanks, back, or thighs.

As a guideline, the price of a **liposuction** can be placed in these ranges:

* Liposuction of a small area: from €3,000
* Liposuction of several areas: between €4,000 and €6,000
* Liposuction plus or full body contouring: from €6,000

These amounts are estimates. The final budget always depends on the surgeon's assessment, the number of areas treated, and the type of result sought.

## Price of liposuction in Spain

The [**The price of liposuction in Spain**](https://clinicabelba.com/precio-liposuccion-espana/) is usually placed illustratively between **€2,500 and €7,500**, although it may vary depending on the extent of the treatment and the technique used, as well as the professional's experience.

Roughly, the most common ranges can be:

* **Small area liposuction**between **€2,500 and €3,500**
* **Liposuction of multiple areas**between **€3,500 and €5,500**
* **Wider liposuction or more complete body contouring**between **€5,500 and €7,500 or more**

These prices are indicative and may change depending on the clinic, city, surgeon, operating room, and included postoperative follow-up.

## Difference Between Liposuction and Liposculpture

Although they are sometimes used as synonyms, they don't always mean exactly the same thing.

### Liposuction

* It focuses on eliminating localized fat.

### Liposuction

* In addition to fat removal, it seeks a more precise body contouring, and in some cases, may include fat grafting to other areas.

This difference can also affect the final price.

## Frequently Asked Questions About Liposuction Prices

### How much does liposuction cost?

The price depends on the area treated, the number of areas, the technique, and the services included. The best way to find out is with a personalized consultation.

### What is the price of liposuction in Spain?

In Spain, the indicative range is usually between **€2,500 and €7,500**, depending on the complexity of the treatment.

### What is the price of liposuction in Barcelona?

In Barcelona, the indicative range is usually between **€3,000 and €8,000**, Depending on the areas and the scope of the surgery.

### The price of a liposuction includes: \* \*\*Surgeon's fees:\*\* This covers the surgeon's expertise, time, and skill. \* \*\*Anesthesiologist's fees:\*\* This covers the anesthesiologist's time and the cost of anesthesia. \* \*\*Operating room fees:\*\* This covers the facility costs, equipment, and staff for the surgery. \* \*\*Pre-operative and post-operative care:\*\* This includes consultations, follow-up appointments, and any necessary medical supplies. \* \*\*Medications:\*\* This covers any prescriptions for pain management or antibiotics. \* \*\*Compression garments:\*\* These are essential for recovery and are usually included.

It usually includes consultation, planning, surgery, anesthesia, operating room, and follow-up appointments, although it depends on each clinic.

### Is liposuction of multiple areas more expensive?

Yes. In general, the more extensive the surgery and the more areas treated, the higher the price usually is.

### Does liposuction work for losing weight?

No. Liposuction is not a weight loss treatment, but rather a surgery to improve body contour by removing localized fat.

### Liposuction and lipo sculpture are two terms for the same procedure. Liposuction is the removal of excess fat deposits from specific areas of the body using suction. Lipo sculpture refers to the process of removing fat and then carefully injecting it into other areas of the body to improve contour and shape.

Liposuction removes localized fat; liposculpture also aims for a more precise reshaping of the silhouette.

### Does the price change depending on the area treated?

Yes. Treating a small area does not cost the same as performing surgery on several body areas at once.
