## Breast Lift Techniques in Barcelona: Which One is Yours?

There are several techniques for performing a breast lift. The professional will decide which is most appropriate based on the condition of your skin, the position of your areola, and the volume of your breasts.

♡

Mastopexy with a circumareolar incision

When the tissue to be removed is minimal and the sagging is not very pronounced, a small incision is made around the areola to reposition the breast. The scar is very discreet, hidden at the edge of the areola.

Ideal for light falls with minimal tissue to remove

* Minimal incision around the areola
* Breast repositioning
* Very discreet scar

✦

Autologous Fat Transfer (Fat Grafting)

The patient's own natural fat is used to provide volume and achieve firm, lifted breasts. It is the most viable option for those who desire natural results, increasing their breast volume only slightly.

Ideal for those seeking naturalness without foreign bodies

* 100% in-house fabric
* Very natural result
* Without implants

Breast augmentation

ϟ

Breast augmentation with implants

If the patient requires implants, they can choose between round ones of different volumes, anatomical ones for a more natural result, and ergonomic ones that adapt to the anatomy. The covering can be silicone or polyurethane, with silicone or saline filling.

Ideal when the breast looks empty and volume is desired

* Round implants
* Anatomical implants
* Ergonomic implants

∿

Breast lift with a vertical scar

The incision surrounds the areola and descends vertically to the inframammary fold. It allows for correction of moderate sagging by reshaping the tissue and reducing the size of the areola if necessary.

Ideal for moderate breast sagging

* Moderate drop correction
* Areola reduction if applicable
* Firm and lasting results

◎

Anchor mastopexy (inverted T)

In cases of volume loss, excess breast tissue, and sagging, an incision is made starting around the areola, going vertically down, and ending at the breast crease, removing excess tissue and reducing the areola.

Ideal for pronounced sagging with excess tissue

* Full correction
* Excision of excess tissue
* Firm and lifted breasts

## Frequently Asked Questions about Breast Lifts in Barcelona

Solve your most common doubts about mastopexy

## The technique

### What is better, fat transfer (your own fat) or implants?

There is no better or worse method; both are valid and adapt to the requirements, expectations, and condition of each patient. Fat transfer is the most viable option for those who desire natural results, increasing their breast volume only slightly. Breast implants provide significant volume, so they are recommended for those who want a more substantial change.

### Can breasts be lifted without surgery?

The answer is no: the way to correct sagging breasts is by performing surgery in which the natural support structure of the breasts is repositioned and excess tissue is removed. Breast lift is the only definitive solution that provides completely guaranteed results.

### What scars does breast augmentation leave?

There are three types of incisions: the periareolar (only around the areola), the vertical (around the areola and downwards), and the anchor, or inverted T (areola, vertical, and breast fold). All can be imperceptible thanks to Clínica Belba's minimal scar technique.

## The process

### What is the pre-operative consultation like?

The preoperative consultation is key: the surgeon assesses the condition of the skin, the position of the areola, and the volume of the breasts, and defines the technique, the necessary tests, and the possible use of implants or autologous fat. The goal is to design a safe, proportionate intervention that is consistent with each patient's body.

### How long does the operation last?

Breast lifts without implants can be performed in an hour and a half. If implants are included, the surgery could take approximately two to three hours, depending on the patient's skin condition and the technique used.

### What anesthesia is used?

General anesthesia is used: the patient will be asleep during surgery to relax her muscles and prevent her from feeling pain. It is administered by a specialist, who monitors the patient's safety throughout the procedure.

## Recovery

### How long is recovery from a breast lift?

The recovery time is approximately one month. During the first seven days, there may be pain, redness, swelling, and some bruising. It is very important to follow the post-operative care and the professional's instructions.

### What type of bra is used post-surgery?

After surgery, a post-surgical or compression bra is usually recommended. It's designed to support the breasts, reduce swelling, and promote proper tissue adaptation during the initial weeks. The exact type of bra and duration of use depend on each individual case and the surgeon's instructions.

### What is the price of a breast lift?

The price of a breast lift in Spain usually ranges approximately between €4,000 and €7,500, with an average close to €5,500. When the surgery includes breast implants to increase volume, the cost is usually higher, starting from €6,500, depending on the clinic, the surgeon, and the technique used.

## Results and scars

### A breast lift with implants is a good option for women who have lost breast volume and firmness due to pregnancy, weight loss, or aging, and who want to restore a more youthful and lifted appearance.

The ideal time is when you have enough time for a quiet recovery. The climate also plays a role: healing is more difficult in hot weather, so spring and autumn are preferable seasons. The final decision is based on the specialist's evaluation.

### Is sensation lost after a breast lift?

In the recovery period, some patients may experience a decrease in sensation due to tissue removal and incision cuts. This is not a permanent condition; normal sensation usually returns after a few months.
