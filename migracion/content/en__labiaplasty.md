Surgeons can perform three types of **Laser labiaplasty**.

### Labiaplasty scars can vary depending on the surgical technique used and the individual's healing process. Generally, the goal of labiaplasty is to create discreet scars that are difficult to see. This placement helps to camouflage the scars. \* \*\*Appearance:\*\* In the initial healing phase, the scars may appear reddish or slightly raised. The ideal time is that which allows for adequate rest. The patient should be able to relax and reduce her daily activities for at least a month. Some women prefer to wait until spring or warmer seasons due to the possibility of wearing more breathable clothing.

### Why is vaginal labia surgery often combined with mons pubis surgery?

Vaginal lip surgery is often combined with [Mons pubis liposuction](https://clinicabelba.com/en/mons-pubis-liposuction/) because both procedures have the same objective, which is the rejuvenation of the genital area.

Labia reduction surgery reduces the size of the labia minora, while mons pubis surgery can remove excess fat and tissue, improve symmetry, and reduce sagging in the upper area of the labia majora and mons pubis.

Together, both procedures can significantly improve the aesthetic appearance of the vaginal area.

### Is it difficult to have orgasms after genital labia surgery?

Technically no. The main nerves of the external genitalia do not run through where the incision is usually made, but at a deeper level. However, it will all depend on the patient's conditions and the specific procedure to be performed.
