## Facelift surgery is indicated when there are signs of aging in the face and neck, such as sagging skin, deep wrinkles, and loss of definition in the jawline and neck. It is typically considered for individuals who have lost elasticity in their skin and want to achieve a more youthful appearance.

Indicated in patients with **stable weight**, **Good general health** y **Realistic expectations** — women and men who:

✓They have signs of aging on their face and neck, generally starting at 45 years old.

✓They have lines at the corners of the mouth and nose, a double chin, or fallen cheekbones.

✓Do you have sagging in your neck or wrinkles on your brow and eyes

✓They are looking for a more permanent solution than cosmetic products.

✓They enjoy good health and have realistic expectations.

✓They maintain healthy lifestyles

Warning**Important:** It is not a weight loss method. It is not recommended if future pregnancies or significant short-term weight changes are planned. The surgeon evaluates each case individually during the first consultation.

How to do it: step-by-step procedure

From Consultation to Recovery: Complete Medical Support

01

Initial Assessment and Planning

Prior consultation: review of medical history, physical examination of the face with photographs from different angles, and discussion of expectations and scope of the procedure.

02

Pre-operative marking and anesthesia

Preoperative: Wash hair and face thoroughly with antiseptics, observe fasting, take only approved medication, arrange for accompanied transport, and plan for days of rest.

03

The intervention: tissue stretching and repositioning

With general anesthesia in the operating room and supervision by an anesthesiologist. On average, about two hours; if the rejuvenation is more complex, it can take about four.

04

Closure, band, and immediate postoperative period

Pain and discomfort controlled with analgesics; inflammation and possible bruising for the first two weeks. Sleep with your head elevated, apply cold compresses, avoid the sun for at least three weeks, and do not engage in intense physical activity during rest.

## What you should know before your facelift: risks and pre-operative information

At Clínica Belba, we believe that medical transparency is part of care. Like any surgical procedure, a facelift involves risks that are important to know before making a decision—and our plastic surgeons will explain them to you in detail during your pre-operative consultation.

**Most frequent** *(they resolve themselves during the first few months in the vast majority of cases)*

* Discomfort or pain in the first few days, controlled with painkillers
* Inflammation and possible bruising the first two weeks
* Numbness or temporary changes in sensation on the face

**Less common**

* Hematomas in the first 24 hours or raised scars
* Nerve injuries, very rare
* Loss of sensation or mobility, hair loss, or tissue loss (rare)

Frequently Asked Questions

## Frequently Asked Questions about Facelifts in Barcelona

Address your most common questions about facelift surgery

### What is the price of a facelift in Barcelona?

It is not a standard value; it is calculated considering each patient's treatment plan. After a personalized medical evaluation, each patient receives the cost of their operation in detail.

### What scars does a facelift leave?

They remain camouflaged behind the ears and in the hairline. Additionally, over time they become less visible.

### Facial rejuvenation surgery is contraindicated in certain situations. These include: \* \*\*Active infections:\*\* If you have any active infections, especially in the face or head area, surgery will be postponed until the infection is cleared. \* \*\*Uncontrolled medical conditions:\*\* Serious uncontrolled medical conditions such as severe heart disease, uncontrolled diabetes, or bleeding disorders can increase surgical risks and may lead to contraindication. \* \*\*Poor wound healing:\*\* Individuals with a history of poor wound healing or keloid formation may be at higher risk for complications. \* \*\*Certain medications:\*\* Blood thinners (anticoagulants) and certain herbal supplements can increase the risk of bleeding and may need to be discontinued before surgery. \* \*\*Unrealistic expectations:\*\* Patients with unrealistic expectations about the outcomes of surgery may not be good candidates. A thorough consultation with the surgeon is crucial to ensure expectations are aligned with what can be achieved. \* \*\*Smoking:\*\* Smoking significantly impairs wound healing and increases the risk of complications. Surgeons often require patients to quit smoking for a specified period before and after surgery. \* \*\*Severe psychological conditions:\*\* Conditions like body dysmorphic disorder can make it difficult for patients to be satisfied with surgical results. \* \*\*Recent radiation therapy to the face:\*\* If you've recently had radiation therapy to the facial area, it can affect tissue healing and may be a contraindication. \* \*\*Allergies to anesthesia or materials:\*\* Known severe allergies to anesthesia or specific materials used in surgery can be a reason to avoid the procedure. It's essential to have a comprehensive discussion with a qualified plastic surgeon to determine if you are a suitable candidate for facial rejuvenation surgery. They will assess your overall health, medical history, and specific goals to make this determination.

People with heart or lung disease, and those with healing problems, diabetics, and smokers are not candidates for a facelift.

### What is a good time for a facelift?

It can be done at any time of year. The most important thing is to have the necessary rest weeks available and authorization from a trusted surgeon.

### How painful is a facelift?

It is normal to experience some level of pain and discomfort after surgery, with swelling, bruising, and numbness during the first few days.

### How long does the pain last?

The pain and discomfort begin to subside after a few days and can disappear completely within two weeks.

### Is sensation lost after surgery?

It is possible to lose sensation in part of the face or feel numbness; it is a temporary symptom that usually disappears once healing is complete. On very rare occasions, it lasts longer.

### How many times can a facelift be done?

Technically there is no limit, but repeating it excessively can make it look unnatural: most surgeons agree that three or four times is the upper limit.

### How long do the results last?

The effect usually lasts more than a decade with proper care. With good habits, another lift may not be required; furthermore, subsequent cosmetic treatments will have better effects.
