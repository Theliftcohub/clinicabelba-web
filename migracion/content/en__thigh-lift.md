## What is a thigh lift?

The **Thigh lift**, also called **thigh lift**, is a surgery in which excess skin and adipose tissue are removed from the inner thighs. In addition to an aesthetic concern, this excess tissue can cause chafing and discomfort when walking: by removing it, you gain **mobility** and visibly more contoured and toned legs.

It is especially indicated after a **Significant weight loss** and when skin has lost elasticity with aging.

How to do it: step-by-step procedure

From Consultation to Recovery: Complete Medical Support

01

Initial Assessment and Planning

Before surgery, we assess the amount of skin and fat on your thighs, review your medical history, and perform a complete pre-operative exam. During this visit, we determine if you need a limited cruroplasty, an extended cruroplasty, or a cruroplasty combined with liposuction.

02

Pre-operative marking and anesthesia

The surgeon marks the skin to be removed and the path of the incision on the thigh, aiming to conceal it in the groin whenever possible. The surgery is performed under general anesthesia, administered and monitored by an anesthesiologist.

03

The intervention: liposuction and skin

La cirugía se inicia con una liposucción de la cara interna del muslo; después se retira el exceso de piel según la técnica elegida —limitada o extendida— y se tensa el contorno. La operación dura entre 2 y 3 horas.

04

Closure, band, and immediate postoperative period

Se cierra la incisión con suturas finas y se coloca una faja de compresión que llevarás unos 30 días. Permanecerás en observación y, según tu caso, alta el mismo día o una noche de ingreso, con revisiones programadas y pautas claras de cuidado de las incisiones.

## Lo que debes saber antes de tu lifting de muslos: riesgos e información preoperatoria

En Clínica Belba creemos que la transparencia médica es parte del cuidado. Como cualquier intervención quirúrgica, la cruroplastia conlleva riesgos que es importante conocer antes de tomar una decisión — y nuestros cirujanos plásticos te los explican con detalle durante la consulta preoperatoria.

**Most frequent** *(they resolve themselves during the first few months in the vast majority of cases)*

* Inflamación y hematomas en los muslos los primeros días
* Sensibilidad alterada temporal en la cara interna del muslo
* Tirantez al caminar o sentarse la primera semana

**Less common**

* Bleeding or hematoma that may require drainage
* Scarring alterations, more frequent in smokers
* Infecciones: se previenen con un preoperatorio completo y cuidados pautados
* Asimetrías o irregularidades del contorno

## Preguntas frecuentes sobre el lifting de muslos

Resuelve tus dudas más comunes sobre la cruroplastia en Barcelona

### How much does a thigh lift cost in Barcelona?

The price of a thigh lift is approximately between €3,500 and €7,000, although it can exceed €8,000 in more complex cases. The final cost depends on the amount of skin and fat to be removed and the type of procedure. The first consultation at Clínica Belba is free.

### A thigh lift leaves scars. The location and extent of the scars depend on the type of thigh lift performed: \* \*\*Inner/Medial Thigh Lift:\*\* This typically results in an inverted "L" or "anchor" shaped scar. The vertical component runs from the groin crease down the inner thigh, and the horizontal component runs along the groin crease. \* \*\*Outer/Lateral Thigh Lift:\*\* This may involve scars that extend from the groin crease around the hip and sometimes into the buttock crease. \* \*\*Mini Thigh Lift:\*\* This usually results in a scar that is limited to the groin crease. The scars are usually red and raised initially but fade and flatten over time, typically becoming less noticeable within a year or two. However, scarring is permanent and its final appearance can vary depending on individual healing and genetics.

It depends on the amount of skin to be removed. In limited thighplasty, the scar is in the groin and gluteal fold, so it's practically invisible. In extended thighplasty, the incision is extended up the inner thigh. The scars fade over the months.

### Can it be combined with liposuction?

Yes, in fact, the surgery usually begins with liposuction of the inner thigh and is completed by removing excess skin, which allows for toning and defining the leg's contour in a single procedure.

### What anesthesia is used and how long does the surgery last?

Thigh lifts are performed under general anesthesia, administered by an anesthesiologist who monitors the patient throughout the procedure. The surgery generally lasts between 2 and 3 hours.

### When are the results seen?

The improvement is immediately visible after the procedure, although it may take several weeks or months for the final result to consolidate, when the inflammation completely disappears.

### How long should you wear the compression garment?

After the surgery, a compression garment is placed, which is recommended to be worn for about 30 days. Additionally, it is important to keep the incisions clean and follow the surgeon's recommendations at each follow-up appointment.

### When can I return to work and sports?

At 7 days post-surgery, work activities that do not involve physical exertion can be resumed. Sports training is authorized after 6 weeks.

### Who shouldn't get a thigh lift?

It is contraindicated in people with cardiovascular or coagulation disorders, those prone to infections, with poorly controlled diabetes, or systemic diseases. In the free medical evaluation, each case is reviewed individually and honestly.
