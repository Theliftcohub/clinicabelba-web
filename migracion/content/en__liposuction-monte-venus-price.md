## Liposuction of the mons pubis is indicated when there is an excess of localized fat in this area that is disproportionate to the rest of the body and causes aesthetic concerns or discomfort. It is a suitable option for individuals who: \* Have good skin elasticity: This allows the skin to retract and adapt to the new contour after fat removal. \* Are generally healthy and do not have conditions that would make surgery risky. \* Have realistic expectations about the results. \* Are bothered by the appearance of a prominent or enlarged mons pubis, which may affect their clothing choices or self-esteem.

Liposuction of the mons pubis may be recommended when there is:

* Excess localized fat in the pubic area
* Visible bulge with tight clothing
* Disproportion between the abdomen and the pubic area

* Changes after pregnancy
* Changes after significant weight loss
* Body image distress or insecurity

It's not always just fat that's the problem. In some cases, there's also sagging, and in those situations, it might be more advisable to combine surgery with **pubic lift**.

## Liposuction of the mons pubis and pubic lift are two different cosmetic procedures. \*\*Liposuction of the mons pubis\*\* is a procedure that removes excess fat from the mons pubis, the fatty mound located above the pubic bone. This can help to reduce the prominence of this area, creating a smoother and more balanced appearance. It is typically performed using small incisions and a suction cannula to remove the fat. \*\*A pubic lift (also known as a monsplasty or pubic lift surgery)\*\* is a surgical procedure that tightens and lifts the skin and underlying tissue of the mons pubis. This procedure is usually performed when there is excess, sagging skin in the pubic area, often due to significant weight loss, aging, or genetics. The surgeon removes excess skin and fat and then tightens the remaining tissue to create a firmer, more lifted appearance. In summary: \* \*\*Liposuction\*\* removes excess fat. \* \*\*Pubic lift\*\* removes excess skin and tightens the area. Sometimes, these procedures can be combined if a patient has both excess fat and excess skin in the pubic region.

Although many people confuse them, they are not exactly the same.

* **Pubic liposuction** It is designed to eliminate localized fat.

* **The pubic lift** it is used when there is laxity or descent of tissues in addition to volume.

In some cases, both procedures can be combined to achieve a more complete and natural result. This difference also influences the **final price of liposuction**.

## What is a mons pubis liposuction like

The intervention is usually performed through small incisions, through which a fine cannula is inserted to suction excess fat in a controlled manner. The goal is not only to reduce volume but also to improve the proportion of the area and achieve a natural result.

Depending on the case, it can be done as a standalone procedure or combined with other body treatments. When greater precision in reshaping is sought, the use of technologies such as [VASER Lipo](https://clinicabelba.com/en/vaser-liposuction/).

## Recovery and postoperative

Recovery depends on each patient, but it's common to experience local swelling and tenderness for the first few days. The postoperative process typically requires:

* Initial relative rest
* Use of the indicated garment, if applicable
* Avoid intense exercise for a while
* Attend medical check-ups

The results begin to be appreciated when the inflammation subsides, although the final result is best evaluated after several weeks.

## Frequently Asked Questions about Mons Pubis Liposuction Price

### How much does a Venus mound liposuction cost?

The price varies depending on the case, technique, anesthesia, and if it is combined with other procedures. The most accurate way to know the price is through a personalized medical evaluation.

### Is liposuction painful?

The procedure is performed under anesthesia, and the postoperative period is generally well tolerated.

### Does the price include anesthesia and the operating room?

It depends on the clinic. That's why it's important to confirm what services are included before comparing quotes.

### Is liposuction of the mons pubis the same as a pubic lift?

No. Liposuction removes fat and a facelift corrects sagging. They can sometimes be combined.

### Can it be done along with other surgeries?

Yes, in some cases, it can be combined with other body or intimate surgery treatments, which can also alter the budget.

### How long is the recovery?

Initial recovery is usually from a few days to a couple of weeks, although full recovery depends on each patient.

### Are the results final?

The fat removed doesn't come back as such, but maintaining a stable weight helps preserve the results better long-term.

### What's the difference between VASER lipo and traditional liposuction?

VASER technology allows for greater precision and less damage to tissues.

### How long does the procedure last?

It usually lasts between 1 and 2 hours, depending on the case.

### Does the fat come back?

No, but it's important to maintain healthy habits.

### Can it be combined with other surgeries?

Yes, it is common to combine it with abdominal or body treatments.
