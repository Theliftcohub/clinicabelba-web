# Author: Dr. Mike Dewever

Inició sus estudios en la Université Libre de Bruxelles y terminó licenciándose en la Universidad de Barcelona y recibiendo el premio extraordinario de licenciatura por sus brillantes resultados académicos. Fue el número 75 del examen MIR, de entre mas de 13.000 participantes. Este logro le permitió realizar su residencia en el Hospital Universitario de Bellvitge. Allí se especializó en cirugía mamaria, plástica y reconstructiva. Al acabar la residencia fue contratado como médico adjunto del servicio de cirugía plástica del mismo Hospital Universitario de Bellvitge. Completó su formación como Cirujano plástico en Barcelona en la Clínica Teknon, y en la Universidad de Columbia, New York. Es autor y co-autor de varias publicaciones nacionales e internacionales. Es miembro de la Secpre y de la SCCP. Sus conocimientos son reconocidos a nivel europeo con el titulo de Fellow of the European Board of Plastic surgery (EBOPRAS 2016).
Desde del 2017 se dedica exclusivamente a la cirugía privada, con unos resultados muy exitosos, pues la satisfacción de sus pacientes avala la excelencia de su trabajo.
Ha participado en varios curso con el Dr Alfredo Hoyos, leader de la cirugia coroporal a nivel mundial. En cirugia mamaria, es pionero en cirugia mamaria minimamente invasiva, con recuperacion rapida y resutaldos naturales, realizando masterclass sobre su tecnica minimal scar.
Ha trabajado durante 4 años en la prestigiosa Clínica Planas, donde ha realizado mas de 600 cirugías, su experiencia acumulada representa mas de 3000 cirugías.

## [There isn't a single "best" age for breast augmentation, as it's a deeply personal decision that depends on individual circumstances and maturity. However, there are some general guidelines and considerations: \*\*Physical Maturity:\*\* \* \*\*Generally, most surgeons recommend that patients be at least 18 years old.\*\* This is because breast development is typically complete by this age. Forcing the issue before full development could potentially lead to complications or suboptimal aesthetic results. \* \*\*Many surgeons prefer patients to be closer to their mid-20s.\*\* By this age, a woman's body is usually fully developed, and her weight has likely stabilized. This provides a more predictable canvas for the surgeon to work with and reduces the risk of changes in breast size or shape due to weight fluctuations after surgery. \*\*Emotional and Psychological Maturity:\*\* \* \*\*The decision should be well-thought-out and not driven by temporary trends or external pressures.\*\* A good candidate for breast augmentation is someone who has realistic expectations about the outcomes and understands the risks and benefits involved. \* \*\*It's important that the desire for breast augmentation stems from a personal desire to feel more confident or comfortable, rather than trying to please someone else or fit a specific ideal.\*\* \*\*Life Circumstances:\*\* \* \*\*It's often advisable to wait until after you've had children, if that's something you plan to do.\*\* Pregnancy and breastfeeding can significantly change the size and shape of the breasts, potentially altering the results of augmentation surgery. While augmentation can be performed before or after children, it's important to discuss these possibilities with your surgeon. \* \*\*Financial readiness and the ability to take time off for recovery are also practical considerations.\*\* \*\*In summary, while 18 is often the minimum age, many surgeons believe that ideally, individuals should wait until their mid-20s or later.\*\* This allows for complete physical development, greater emotional maturity, and a more stable life situation, leading to more informed and satisfying results. The most important thing is to have a thorough consultation with a board-certified plastic surgeon who can assess your individual situation, discuss your goals, and provide personalized guidance.](https://clinicabelba.com/en/mejor-edad-para-aumento-pecho/)
