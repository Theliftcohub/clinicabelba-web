## Areola and Nipple Reduction: What It Is and How It's Done

Areola reduction (areoloplasty) is one of the most rewarding breast surgeries: small, fast, and with a great aesthetic impact. Here's everything you need to know.

♡

What is areola reduction surgery?

Breast areola reduction decreases its size and the nipple's size if necessary. It is performed to improve symmetry between both areolas, reduce excess skin, correct congenital asymmetries, or perfect the results after breast augmentation.

Ideal for areolas disproportionate to the breast

* Symmetrical areolas
* Less excess skin
* Also after an increase

✦

When is this surgery recommended?

When the size of the areola is disproportionate to the dimensions of the breast, as occurs with hypertrophic nipples (macrotelia): increased nipple length, width, and depth. Also in cases of asymmetry between the two areolas or excess skin around them.

Ideal for hypertrophic nipples and asymmetries

* Hypertrophic nipples
* Asymmetries between areolas
* Excess skin around

3 techniques

ϟ

Techniques: nipple, areola, or combined

For the nipple, the circumference under the skin (thickness) is reduced, or a section of the tip (length) is removed. For the areola, the inner or outer part is resected. If the areola is very large, the lollipop technique is used: an incision around the areola and vertically towards the fold. The techniques can be combined.

The technique is chosen according to your anatomy during the consultation.

* Nipple reduction
* Areola resection
* Lollipop technique

∿

Local anesthesia and 45–60 minutes

Performed independently, it is considered minor surgery: local instead of general anesthesia and an outpatient procedure lasting between 45 and 60 minutes. You go home the same day and recovery is one of the fastest in breast surgery.

Outpatient surgery: home the same day

* No income

Results: symmetrical and natural areolas

The result is symmetrical areolas and a more aesthetic, youthful-looking breast: small areolas are associated with younger age. Psychologically, this translates into increased self-esteem and a better perception of physical appearance.

Youthful and well-proportioned chest appearance.

## Risks of Areola Reduction: What You Need to Know

As with any surgical intervention, the risks of an areola reduction are generally modest and are considerably reduced with proper care before making a decision.

## Frequently Asked Questions about Areola Reduction

Resolve your doubts about areoloplasty

## The technique

### What is areola reduction surgery (areoloplasty)?

It is a surgical procedure that reduces the size of the areola and, if necessary, the nipple itself. It is performed to improve symmetry between both areolas, reduce excess skin, correct congenital asymmetries, or perfect the results after breast augmentation.

### What techniques exist?

Three: nipple reduction (the circumference under the skin is reduced or a section of the tip is removed), areola resection (the inner or outer part is removed), and, for very large areolas, the lollipop technique, with an incision around the areola and vertically towards the fold. They can also be combined.

### What is macrotelia?

It is the medical term for excessively large or hypertrophic nipples: increased nipple length, width, and depth, often with increased areola depth. It is one of the main indications for this surgery.

## The process

### How much does an areola reduction cost?

The price typically ranges from approximately €1,290 to €2,500. It varies depending on the clinic, the surgeon's experience, and whether the procedure is performed alone or in combination with other breast surgeries, such as breast augmentation or lift. The quote is personalized after the consultation.

### What anesthesia is used and how long does it last?

When performed independently, it is considered a minor intervention: local anesthesia and between 45 and 60 minutes, on an outpatient basis. If combined with other surgeries, it may require general anesthesia.

### What is the prior consultation like?

The surgeon evaluates the shape and size of the areola, the condition of the skin and breast tissue, listens to your aesthetic concerns, informs you of the risks and expected results, and orders necessary clinical exams.

## Recovery

### How long is the recovery?

It is one of the fastest breast surgeries: recovery at home from the very beginning, return to work in 2-3 days, and full recovery in about 15 days, depending on care.

### What precautions should be taken?

Wear a well-fitting and comfortable bra day and night, avoid sports and lifting weights until medically cleared, and avoid sexual arousal for the first week to reduce swelling of the breast tissue.

### What is a good time to have surgery?

When you have availability to recover comfortably. It is usually recommended to avoid summer, as high temperatures can cause greater discomfort.

## Results and scars

### What scars does it leave?

The scar remains around the outer edge of the areola and is perfectly disguised thanks to the techniques used. Over time, it fades to become practically invisible, although healing varies from patient to patient.

### Is sensation lost?

Usually not. There can be a temporary loss of sensation during recovery, but it's rarely permanent. Some patients notice a change in areola pigmentation, though this is quite unusual.

### Can it be combined with other surgeries?

Yes, it's common to combine it with breast augmentation with implants or a breast lift. In that case, general anesthesia is used, and the quote is personalized according to the treatment plan.
