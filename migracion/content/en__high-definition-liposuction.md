## What is high-definition liposuction (HD lipo)?

The **High-definition liposuction**, also called **HD lipo, high-definition liposculpture, or 4D liposuction**, is an advanced technique that eliminates unwanted fat with cutting-edge technology and a more precise method than traditional liposuction.

Your goal is not to lose weight: it is **accentuate the figure and achieve anatomical definition** from each area, enhancing the underlying muscles without damaging the surrounding tissues.

How to do it: step-by-step procedure

From Consultation to Recovery: Complete Medical Support

01

Initial Assessment and Planning

Pre-consultation: We evaluate your muscle anatomy, body fat percentage, and skin quality. HD lipo requires a good baseline fitness level. During this visit, we confirm you are a good candidate and set your definition goals.

02

Pre-operative marking and anesthesia

The surgeon marks the muscular anatomy to be enhanced on the skin—linea alba, rectus, obliques. The procedure is performed under local anesthesia and, if necessary, light sedation to keep you relaxed.

03

The intervention: ultrasound and sculpture

High-frequency ultrasound selectively liquefies fat, which is then extracted through minimal incisions using a fine cannula, sculpting the contour over the muscle. It lasts between 1 and 6 hours depending on the number of areas.

04

Closure, band, and immediate postoperative period

Compression garment, abundant hydration, and rest: a minimum of 2 to 4 days off (7-10 recommended) and no intense exercise for 2 weeks. Results are visible once swelling subsides, with scheduled follow-ups.

## What you should know before your HD lipo: risks and pre-operative information

At Clínica Belba, we believe that medical transparency is part of care. Like any surgical intervention, high-definition liposuction carries risks that are important to know before making a decision—and our plastic surgeons explain them to you in detail during your preoperative consultation.

**Most frequent** *(they resolve themselves during the first few months in the vast majority of cases)*

* Swelling and bruising in the treated areas for the first few days
* Discomfort when moving the first week, controlled with analgesics
* Altered temporary sensation in the carved area

**Less common**

* Bleeding or hematoma that may require drainage
* Infection: prevented with hospital protocol and scheduled wound care
* Contour irregularities, less than with traditional liposuction due to the precision of the technique
* Reactions to medications or anesthetics: are ruled out in the pre-operative evaluation

Frequently Asked Questions

## Frequently Asked Questions About High-Definition Liposuction

Resolve your most common questions about HD liposuction in Barcelona

### What is the price of HD liposuction in Barcelona?

It is approximately between €3,990 and €10,000 depending on the areas, complexity, technology, and surgeon's experience. A full tummy tuck usually exceeds €6,000. The first consultation at Clínica Belba is free.

### What scars does high-definition liposuction leave?

Very small, almost invisible, at the points where the incisions are made. This is one of the advantages of the technique: it extracts fat with precision without damaging the surrounding tissues.

### High-definition liposculpture is contraindicated in several situations:

It is not a solution for weight loss, nor is it recommended for individuals with excessively high body fat percentages. The ideal scenario is to start with a BMI within the recommended range and in good health.

### What anesthesia is used and how long does it last?

Local anesthesia is usually used in the area to be treated, with light sedation if necessary. The average duration ranges from 1 to 6 hours, depending on the number of areas treated.

### How long does the pain last afterwards?

Generally between 1 and 2 weeks. The first week there is discomfort when moving, which is well managed with the painkillers indicated by the doctor.

### Is sensation lost in the treated area?

Some patients notice a slight, temporary loss of sensation in the treated area, which gradually recovers.

### How much leave do I need?

A minimum of 2 to 4 days of rest from work, although 7 to 10 days of rest is recommended. No intense exercise for at least 2 weeks.

### How many times can you have HD liposuction?

There is no fixed limit, as long as it is performed in different areas and with the appropriate medical evaluation in each case.

### Can extracted fat be reused?

Yes. The liposuctioned fat can be purified and injected into other areas to correct defects or add volume (lipofilling), for example in the buttocks or breasts.

### HD Lipo or traditional liposuction?

Traditional liposuction removes fat volume; HD lipo further sculpts the contour to enhance musculature. If you're looking for athletic definition and toned muscles, HD is the right choice.
