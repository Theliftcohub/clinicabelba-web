## What is body surgery?

Body surgery, also called body contouring surgery, **[plastic surgery](https://clinicabelba.com/primera-consulta-cirugia-estetica/)** Body contouring surgery encompasses surgical procedures aimed at improving silhouette, correcting sagging, eliminating stubborn localized fat, reshaping specific areas, or restoring proportions after significant body changes.

Can include procedures such as liposuction, Vaser liposuction, high-definition liposuction, abdominoplasty, **[rib remodeling](https://clinicabelba.com/en/rib-remodeling/)****,** buttock augmentation, arm lift, **[Thigh lift](https://clinicabelba.com/en/thigh-lift/)**, gynecomastia, or mommy makeover, always with individualized medical indication.

The goal is not to artificially transform the body, but to improve its balance, contour, and harmony while respecting each person's anatomy. Before recommending any treatment, our **[plastic surgeons](https://clinicabelba.com/en/plastic-surgeons-barcelona/)** They assess fat distribution, skin quality, laxity, musculature, patient goals, and the realistic possibilities of each technique.

## What You Should Know Before Your Body Surgery: Risks and Preoperative Information

At Clínica Belba, we believe medical transparency is part of care. Like any surgical intervention, body surgery carries risks that are important to know before making a decision – and our plastic surgeons explain them to you in detail during the preoperative consultation.

**Most frequent** *(they resolve themselves during the first few months in the vast majority of cases)*

* Inflammation and bruising in the operated area
* Temporary changes in skin sensitivity
* Transient asymmetry or irregularities during recovery
* Seroma - fluid accumulation, controllable with medical monitoring

**Less common**

* Skin fibrosis or irregularities, reduced with proper technique and correct use of a compression garment
* Infection, treatable with the corresponding medical protocol.
* Deep vein thrombosis — low risk, reduced with standard preventive measures
* Hypertrophic or keloid scar, depending on the individual predisposition of each patient

### What is the difference between liposuction and tummy tuck?

Liposuction removes localized fat in patients with good skin quality. Abdominoplasty is indicated when there is excess skin, abdominal laxity, or separation of abdominal muscles (diastasis) that liposuction cannot correct. In some cases, both are performed in the same procedure.

### What is the difference between liposuction and VASER Lipo?

Both eliminate localized fat, but the [**Lipo Vaser**](https://clinicabelba.com/en/vaser-liposuction/) It uses ultrasound to treat fat more precisely, allowing for more defined body contouring in selected patients.

### Does body surgery help with weight loss?

No. Body plastic surgery is not a weight loss treatment. It is indicated for patients with stable weight who want to correct localized fat, sagging, or excess skin that does not improve with diet or exercise.

### Can I combine multiple body procedures in a single surgery?

Yes. In many cases, it is possible to combine procedures—for example, abdominoplasty with liposuction, or gluteal augmentation with Vaser liposuction of the waist—as long as the medical team considers it safe for the specific case.

### Can body surgery be combined with breast surgery?

Yes. The [**Mommy Makeover**](https://clinicabelba.com/en/mommy-makeover-barcelona/) it's precisely that: a planned combination of body and breast procedures.

### Recovery from abdominoplasty typically takes several weeks to a few months. Most people can return to light activities within 1-2 weeks, but strenuous exercise and heavy lifting should be avoided for 4-6 weeks or longer. Full recovery and final results can take up to a year.

Initial recovery is usually between 2 to 4 weeks for most patients. Final results can take up to 6 months.
