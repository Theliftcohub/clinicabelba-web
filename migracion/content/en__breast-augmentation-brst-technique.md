# BRST Technique Breast Augmentation

The BRST (Best Results Short Time) technique applied to breast augmentation is a surgical approach that refines classic breast surgery methods to make them more efficient and less invasive. It is characterized by reduced procedure times, more precise incisions, and a faster recovery, always maintaining safety standards and the quality of the results. It uses state-of-the-art implants and, in many cases, allows for same-day discharge.

## Breast Augmentation BRST Technique: What it is and what it offers

BRST is not a completely distinct “magical” surgical technique from traditional augmentation mammoplasty; it is a protocol (Best Results Short Time) that optimizes several known phases: planning, anesthesia/sedation, incision, hemostasis, pocket creation, implant placement, and closure.

♡

BRST breast augmentation technique: what does it involve?

With the BRST technique, we aim for natural results by reducing friction at every step: less downtime, instruments designed for efficiency, and an assisted care pathway that avoids unnecessary hospital admissions when they don't add value. It's not a shortcut to increase three sizes at once, nor a promise of zero discomfort; it's a more efficient way to perform surgery that we already know well.

“BRST technique is speed with method, not haste without control”

* fewer dead times
* Instrument that speeds up
* Efficient care pathway

✦

Real benefits: time, scarring, and return to routine

The operating room usually revolves around the hour mark, thanks to a highly choreographed sequence and decisions made beforehand: precise marking, decided implant, and defined plan. When that preparation is well finalized, the intraoperative phase flows and it shows.

Limitations and expectations: moderate increases

What the BRST technique gives you in efficiency, it asks for in prudence. If you are looking for very large increases or to significantly lift the breasts (marked ptosis), the classic plan or a combination with mastopexy will likely be more honest. BRST is designed for moderate increases (1–2 cup sizes) and a result that prioritizes naturalness and comfort in daily life.

“It's not a VIP pass to excessive increases, but a quick and orderly path to a natural result.”

* Increases of 1–2 sizes
* Naturalness and comfort
* Honest indication

◎

BRST vs. Conventional Raise

When we prioritize naturalness and a smooth return to routine without surprises, the BRST technique often wins. If significant hair loss needs to be corrected or a lot of volume is desired, the classic or combined approach is preferred. Anesthesia is individualized: deep sedation with tumescent local anesthesia or short general anesthesia, always with written consent.

The decision is individualized according to security, foreseen time, and team preferences.

* Individualized anesthesia
* Safety first
* Alternatives when applicable

Ideal candidates and common contraindications

The best candidates for breast augmentation share several traits: mild or nonexistent ptosis, expectations of moderate augmentation, skin with minimal laxity, and good general health. BMI, habits (smoking), pregnancy and breastfeeding history, and, above all, the balance between the breast base and the chosen implant width are all considered.

Relative contraindications: uncontrolled coagulation disorders, active infections, expectations incompatible with the BRST framework, or asymmetries requiring additional maneuvers. None of these are an absolute “no,” but they lead to considering alternatives or sequences: first correction, then volume.

First 48 hours and first week

The first 24–48 hours are for pain control and gentle mobility (getting up, walking at home), hydration, sleeping on your back, and wearing the post-operative bra. Between day 3 and 7, many patients describe a decrease in chest tightness and fatigue; desk work is permitted if there is no significant pain and the commute is short.

Weeks 2 to 6: gradual movement and sport

Weeks 2–3: We broaden shoulder range of motion and reintroduce light tasks. Weeks 4–6: We begin a gradual return to sport—first light cardio, then strength training without direct chest work, and finally, chest exercises.

Warning signs and scarring

Warning signs we always review: sudden asymmetry with pain, spreading redness, persistent fever, abnormal drainage, or sudden worsening of pain. We prefer to over-consult rather than fall short. We also review the scar: sun protection, massage if indicated, and silicone when appropriate.

Following the script: keys to success

“Agile” should not be confused with “free will.” BRST's success in recovery lies in following the script: bra, wound hygiene, active pauses, and not advancing milestones (especially strength and impact).

## The technique

### The BRST technique in breast augmentation refers to using a specialized surgical approach that involves creating a pocket for the implant through the armpit (axilla) instead of the traditional inframammary fold (under the breast) or areola. This aims to leave no visible scarring on the breast itself, offering a more discreet result.

The BRST breast augmentation technique consists of an advanced procedure based on precise and personalized surgical planning. Its objective is to improve breast volume and shape while maximally respecting the patient's anatomy, seeking a natural, harmonious, and balanced result with the body.

### What are the advantages of breast augmentation with the BRST technique compared to other techniques?

Among its main advantages are the customization of the procedure, better adaptation of the implant to the patient's anatomy, and an approach focused on more natural-looking results. Furthermore, the BRST technique allows for optimized recovery and reduced surgical impact when well-indicated.

### Does breast augmentation with the BRST technique offer natural results?

Yes. One of the main goals of the BRST technique is to achieve a natural-looking breast, both in shape and proportion. The choice of implant, placement plane, and surgical technique are adapted to each patient to obtain an aesthetic and balanced result.

## The process

### How long does the BRST technique operation take?

The operating room usually takes about an hour, thanks to a very choreographed sequence and decisions made beforehand: precise marking, decided implant, and defined plan.

### What anesthesia is used?

Some teams opt for deep sedation with an anesthesiologist and local tumescent anesthesia; others, for short general anesthesia. There are no dogmas: the decision is individualized based on safety, expected time, and team preferences, and is consented to in writing.

### What is the price of a BRST breast augmentation?

The BRST technique, also known as “permanent push-up,” typically costs between €4,000 and €6,000 in Barcelona. This includes implants, operating room, anesthesia, initial medication, bra, and follow-up appointments, with flexible financing and a fixed price after medical evaluation.

## Recovery

### How long does recovery take after breast augmentation with the BRST technique?

Recovery is usually progressive. In most cases, the patient can resume light daily activities within a few days, wearing a post-surgical bra for several weeks. Intense physical exercise is gradually reintroduced, and definitive results are usually seen between 2 and 3 months.

### When can I return to sports?

In weeks 2-3, range of motion is expanded and light tasks are reintroduced; in weeks 4-6, the gradual return to sports begins: first light cardio, then strength training without direct pectoral work, and finally pectoral workouts.

### Who is the ideal candidate for a BRST breast augmentation?

Any woman who wishes to increase breast volume, improve breast shape, or correct asymmetry is a good candidate, provided she is in good health and has realistic expectations. The suitability of the BRST technique is determined after a personalized medical evaluation by a specialist surgeon.
