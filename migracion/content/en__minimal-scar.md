## Minimally Invasive Breast Augmentation: How the Minimal Scar Technique Works

Thanks to the Minimal Scar methodology, patients can increase their breast volume and achieve a natural décolletage with less visible scars: the minimally invasive evolution of breast augmentation, the most practiced surgery in Spain.

♡

What is breast augmentation with minimal scarring?

This is a minimally invasive procedure that increases breast size through an incision smaller than 3 cm. The goal is to achieve a natural breast contour with very small scars. This method is also applicable to breast lifts with minimal scarring.

Ideal for a light, discreet, and natural breast augmentation.

* Minor 3 cm incision
* Natural neckline
* Barely noticeable scars

✦

When is minimally invasive augmentation recommended?

It is the most suitable option when a natural-looking breast augmentation with imperceptible scars is desired: the perfect procedure for those who want a discreet and moderate volume décolletage. It is also used for breast firming and to correct breasts that have sagged due to aging, weight loss, or pregnancy.

Ideal for discreet and moderate volume necklines

* Natural finish
* Moderate volume
* Breast augmentation

Exclusive Technique

ϟ

Minimal Scar technique step-by-step

A small incision of 2.5 cm is made at the lower part of the mammary gland. The surgical area is illuminated with special devices and a sleeve is inserted into the incision, which displaces the prosthesis inside the breast without being manipulated by the surgeon, thus reducing the risk of contamination.

Incision from 2.5 cm in the inframammary fold

* Incision from 2.5 cm
* Prosthetics without direct manipulation
* Lower risk of contamination

∿

Ergonomic breast implants

This surgery requires ergonomic implants: a soft shell and cohesive silicone gel filling that conforms to the breast's silhouette. They are easier to fold and insert through a minimal incision and change shape with movement, reducing the risk of capsular contracture and late seromas.

Ideal for naturally defined breasts

* Adaptable cohesive gel
* Less capsular contracture
* Comfort on the go

◎

Results: a light, firm, and natural bust

The results are visualized with a more defined proportion of the breast contour, and lifted, firm breasts. The procedure is performed under general anesthesia supervised by an anesthesiologist, and the operating room time usually does not exceed 90 minutes.

Defined contour with very small scars

* Supervised general anesthesia
* Less than 90 minutes in the operating room
* Definitive and natural results

## How to Prepare for Your Minimally Invasive Surgery: Step-by-Step

We guide you step-by-step to ensure your breast surgery is completely calm and safe.

In the first consultation, the surgeon assesses your skin texture, bone structure, current breast size, and the position of your nipple and areola. They will explain whether breast augmentation with minimal scarring is the right procedure for your expectations: implant options, technique to be used, surgery duration, and potential complications.

The relevant tests are: chest X-rays, mammography, blood tests, and electrocardiogram. In the days prior: rest, adhere to the fasting prescribed by the anesthesiologist, discontinue medications that affect coagulation, and if you smoke, avoid tobacco for 4 weeks before the procedure.

The surgery is performed under general anesthesia, constantly supervised by the anesthesiologist. Thanks to surgical innovations, the time in the operating room usually does not exceed 90 minutes. Personalized surgical marking before entering.

## Risks of Minimally Invasive Breast Augmentation: What You Need to Know

As with any surgical intervention, the Minimal Scar technique reduces the risks of capsular contracture and infections, although like any surgery, it carries risks that are important to know before making a decision.

Risks reduced by design

The 3 cm incision and sterile sleeve make this augmentation safer than conventional methods.

* Capsular contracture: lower risk with ergonomic implants
* Infection: implant enters without direct contact
* Late seromas: reduced by lower friction
* Minimal scar in the breast fold

Possible (transient) discomfort

Like any surgery, there can be temporary, controllable effects.

* Allergic reactions or incision infection
* Swelling and bruising from manipulation
* Transient loss of sensation
* They are resolved by team tracking

## Frequently Asked Questions about Minimally Invasive Breast Augmentation

Resolve your doubts about the Minimal Scar technique

## The technique

### What is breast augmentation with minimal scarring?

This is an intervention where breast dimensions are increased through an incision less than 3 cm, aiming for a natural breast contour and very small scars. This minimally invasive method is also applicable to breast lifts with minimal scarring.

### Is a breast augmentation possible with a 3 cm scar?

Yes. Scientific and technological advancements allow for breast augmentation with 3 cm incisions thanks to the design and malleability of ergonomic breast implants, which are easier to bend and insert through a minimal incision.

### What is better, fat grafting (lipotransfer) or ergonomic implants?

Fat transfer is an alternative for those who want a subtle change in volume, although it requires having an adequate amount of fat. Ergonomic implants offer more significant volume with a very natural result, similar to fat transfer. The choice is decided in consultation with the surgeon.

## The process

### What is the prior consultation like?

The surgeon will assess the skin texture, bone structure, current breast size, and the position of the nipple and areola. They will explain whether a minimal scar lift or augmentation is the right procedure for your expectations: implant options, technique, procedure time, and potential complications.

### What anesthesia is used and how long does the surgery last?

The patient remains under general anesthesia, supervised by the anesthesiologist throughout the procedure. Thanks to surgical innovations, the operating room time usually does not exceed 90 minutes.

### What is the price of a Minimal Scar surgery in Barcelona?

The cost depends on several factors: clinical expenses, implant prices, bandages, medical team fees, pre-operative tests, and post-operative check-ups. When choosing where to have your surgery, don't just focus on the cost: evaluate the quality of service, personalized attention, the medical team's experience, and the results.

## Recovery

### How long is the recovery?

It is normal to feel discomfort, swelling, and bruising in the first few hours; these are managed with painkillers. For the first 7 days, you should not raise your arms or exert yourself, and for 4 weeks, you must sleep on your back and wear a post-surgical bra. Avoiding alcohol, tobacco, and sun promotes healing.

### What is a good time to have surgery?

The ideal is one where you have enough time to recover peacefully. The date will also depend on the plastic surgeon's prior evaluation, which will determine if the conditions are right to carry it out.

### What does it feel like to have augmented breasts with minimal scarring?

After surgery, there may be pain and some discomfort that disappear over time, until you get used to the new volume and weight. On a psychological level, the change translates into increased self-esteem, greater confidence, and emotional well-being.

## Results and scars

### What scars does surgery leave?

In Minimal Scar breast augmentation, incisions are made in the breast fold and are less than 3 cm, making the scar very small and imperceptible. In a breast lift, they can vary depending on complexity: around the areola or in an anchor shape.

### Is sensation lost after surgery?

Inflammation and bruising can cause a temporary loss of sensation in the area, which gradually disappears during the recovery period.

### Can I get a small, natural-looking breast augmentation?

Yes: it is precisely the ideal indication for the Minimal Scar technique. A slight and discreet augmentation, proportionate to your body, with ergonomic implants that move naturally and an imperceptible scar.
