## What is lobuloplasty? Earlobe repair and reduction

The ear has a lot to say in the harmony of the facial contour: when the lobe is **torn, stretched, or too bulky**, the area loses its balance. We perform lobuloplasty to **patch holes**, reduce lobes elongated by the weight of earrings or by expanders, and **refine voluminous lobes**.

Everything is solved in a way **on an outpatient basis and under local anesthesia**, . without hospitalization.

Treatments

Lobuloplasty techniques performed at Belba Clinic

According to the case, we use **wedge resection, skin flaps, or volumetric reduction**, with very well-hidden scars placed in the natural folds of the earlobe.

## Lobuloplasty is indicated for patients with earlobe fissures or tears, often caused by wearing heavy earrings or trauma. It can also be used to correct stretched or enlarged earlobes from gauge piercings.

Indicated in patients with **stable weight**, **Good general health** y **Realistic expectations** — women and men who:

✓They have torn or very elongated earlobes from heavy earrings or an accidental pull

✓They wore expanders and want to reconstruct the edge of the lobe.

✓They have bulging lobes or asymmetries between lobes

✓They need to redo a failed previous repair.

✓They want to wear earrings again after a lobe repair (with a new piercing).

✓They maintain healthy lifestyles

Warning**Important:** It is not a weight loss method. It is not recommended if future pregnancies or significant short-term weight changes are planned. The surgeon evaluates each case individually during the first consultation.

## Postoperative care and recovery for lobuloplasty

Indicated in patients with **stable weight**, **Good general health** y **Realistic expectations** — women and men who:

✓Clean and dry area for the first 48–72 hours, with the indicated ointment.

✓Antibiotics and painkillers for at least a week, as prescribed.

✓Avoid traction: cell phones, helmets, or tight masks

✓Office the next day; sport picking up steam after the first week

✓Sleep on your back or on your side for the first week.

✓Re-piercing at 6–8 weeks, after medical discharge

Warning**Important:** It is not a weight loss method. It is not recommended if future pregnancies or significant short-term weight changes are planned. The surgeon evaluates each case individually during the first consultation.

Medical Transparency

## What You Should Know Before Your Lobuloplasty: Risks and Information

At Clínica Belba, we believe medical transparency is part of care. Like any surgical procedure, lobuloplasty carries risks that are important to know before making a decision—and our plastic surgeons will explain them to you in detail during your pre-operative consultation.

**Most frequent** *(they resolve themselves during the first few months in the vast majority of cases)*

* Mild discomfort, controllable with mild pain relievers
* Slight local inflammation in the first few days
* Check-ups at 7–14 days and final discharge

**Less common**

* Mild hematoma or infection
* Visible scar on blemish-prone skin
* Asymmetries if the previous tissue was very damaged (very large dilations)

Frequently Asked Questions

## Frequently Asked Questions about Lobuloplasty

Resolve your most common questions about lobe surgery

### Does labiaplasty hurt? Is it done with local anesthesia?

Yes, it's done with local anesthesia. The discomfort is low and manageable with mild painkillers.

### Is the scar noticeable after lobuloplasty?

It usually looks very discreet, located in folds and with fine suturing. With good care, it integrates well within 2–3 months.

### Can I wear earrings again after my earlobes have been reconstructed?

Yes, but not on the scar line: we usually recommend waiting 6–8 weeks and doing a new hole slightly offset.

### How much does a lobuloplasty cost in Barcelona?

Between €450 and €900 per ear as an estimate, with an average cost of around €650 per lobe. It depends on the case (simple tear vs. reconstruction with expanders, unilateral or bilateral); personalized quote after evaluation.

### Is there an age limit for lobuloplasty?

There is no fixed maximum age: we evaluate each case individually based on the condition of the tissue and overall health.

### When can I go back to work and play sports?

Office or remote work the next day if feeling well; physical labor or work requiring a hard hat from week 1-2; gym gradually after the first week.
