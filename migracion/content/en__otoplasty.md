The **Otoplasty** It is an ear surgery that allows the correction of the well-known **Ears that stick out, prominent ears, or protruding ears** placing them in their positions.

## When is ear surgery indicated?

Indicated in patients with **stable weight**, **Good general health** y **Realistic expectations** — women and men who:

✓They have prominent, sticking-out, or winged ears.

✓They have asymmetries, folded ears, or cupped ears.

✓They have suffered an injury or are not comfortable with the shape or size

✓They have completed ear development (since age 5)

✓They seek to improve their aesthetics and increase their personal safety.

✓They maintain healthy lifestyles

Warning**Important:** It is not a weight loss method. It is not recommended if future pregnancies or significant short-term weight changes are planned. The surgeon evaluates each case individually during the first consultation.

How to do it: step-by-step procedure

From Consultation to Recovery: Complete Medical Support

01

Initial Assessment and Planning

Pre-consultation: physical examination and interview to review the patient's medical history and expectations, address questions, and explain the surgery and its results.

02

Pre-operative marking and anesthesia

Pre-operative: no alcohol or tobacco before the procedure, only medication authorized by the surgeon, and days of rest and support arranged for leaving the clinic.

03

The intervention: cartilage resection, remodeling, or folding

With local anesthesia and sedation (or general anesthesia in small or anxious patients), it lasts between 1 and 2 hours; in complex reconstructions, it can extend to 3.

04

Closure, band, and immediate postoperative period

Apply a compression bandage for the first three days, then a headband until the stitches are removed. The surgeon will prescribe painkillers and antibiotics; keep the incisions clean and dry, and avoid contact sports for one month.

## Postoperative care and recovery from otoplasty

Indicated in patients with **stable weight**, **Good general health** y **Realistic expectations** — women and men who:

✓Compression bandage for the first three days

✓Antibiotics and painkillers for at least a week, as prescribed.

✓Headband until stitches are removed

✓No contact physical activities for one month

✓Clean, dry incisions; postpone hair washing for a few days

✓Loose clothing around the neck and attention to wound healing

Warning**Important:** It is not a weight loss method. It is not recommended if future pregnancies or significant short-term weight changes are planned. The surgeon evaluates each case individually during the first consultation.

## What You Should Know Before Your Otoplasty: Risks and Preoperative Information

At Clínica Belba, we believe medical transparency is part of care. Like any surgical procedure, otoplasty carries risks that are important to know before making a decision—and our plastic surgeons will explain them to you in detail during your preoperative consultation.

**Most frequent** *(they resolve themselves during the first few months in the vast majority of cases)*

* Discomfort, itching, or pain in the first few days, managed with painkillers.
* Temporary swelling, numbness, or redness
* Temporary change in the sensitivity of one or both ears

**Less common**

* Healing problems and infections due to poor incision care
* Allergic reaction to points or medications
* If the deformity is not completely corrected, a second operation may be necessary.

Frequently Asked Questions

## Frequently Asked Questions about Otoplasty in Barcelona

Solve your most common questions about ear surgery

### What is the real cost of ear surgery in Barcelona?

The value depends on the surgeon's planning and the necessary medical and instrumental resources. The price is personalized: as a guideline, it ranges between €1,900 and €4,000, with an average cost of €2,500 to €2,900.

### Otoplasty leaves minimal scarring. The incisions are typically made behind the ear, in the natural crease, rendering the scars inconspicuous. In some cases, small incisions may be made on the front of the ear, which also tend to fade well.

They are located at the back of the ear, so they are not noticeable. They are precise incisions that promote healing and fade over time.

### Reconstructive ear surgery is contraindicated in individuals who have certain medical conditions or have had a history of prior surgeries that could compromise healing or increase the risk of complications. Specific contraindications may include active infections, certain autoimmune disorders, and a history of radiation therapy to the head and neck region.

Not allowed for people with hypertension, heart or respiratory problems, smokers, diabetics, obese individuals, or those with any health condition that compromises healing and recovery after anesthesia.

### When is a good time for ear surgery?

Otoplasty has an average recovery time of two weeks, so any time of year can be favorable for the procedure.

### How painful is an otoplasty?

Most patients report moderate to mild pain during the first few days, which is managed with pain medication prescribed by the surgeon.

### How long does the pain last?

It can last from several days to weeks. It's common to experience swelling, numbness, itching, or redness, especially in the first few days after surgery.

### Is sensation lost after surgery?

It is normal for there to be a change in the sensitivity of one or both ears; however, it is a temporary symptom.

### An otoplasty can be performed multiple times. There isn't a strict limit on the number of times the procedure can be done.

Most patients only need one procedure to achieve permanent and satisfactory results. In rare cases, a second otoplasty may be necessary.
