## Breast Reduction in Barcelona: What It Is, When, and How It's Done

Breast reduction surgery is one of the most satisfying procedures: it improves health, relieves back pain, and harmonizes the figure. Here's everything you need to know.

♡

What is breast reduction surgery?

Large breasts are usually hereditary or hormonal in origin and, beyond aesthetics, cause irritation, back pain, and skin distension. Breast reduction removes excess fat and breast tissue, raises the position of the breasts, and, if necessary, reduces the size of the areola to achieve your ideal size.

Ideal for reducing two or three sizes and relieving back pain

* Less volume and weight
* Elevated and symmetrical chest
* Areola reduction if applicable

✦

Breast reduction is recommended when a person experiences physical discomfort or health problems due to large breasts. This can include:
\* \*\*Chronic back, neck, and shoulder pain:\*\* The weight of large breasts can strain these areas, leading to persistent pain.
\* \*\*Difficulty with physical activity:\*\* Large breasts can make exercise uncomfortable or even painful, limiting participation in sports and other activities.
\* \*\*Skin irritation and rashes:\*\* The skin under the breasts can become irritated, red, and prone to rashes due to friction and moisture.
\* \*\*Nerve compression or carpal tunnel-like symptoms:\*\* In some cases, the weight of the breasts can put pressure on nerves, leading to numbness or tingling in the arms and hands.
\* \*\*Grooves from bra straps:\*\* Deep indentations from bra straps can be uncomfortable and indicative of the strain on the shoulders.
\* \*\*Self-consciousness or psychological distress:\*\* Beyond physical symptoms, some individuals experience significant emotional distress or low self-esteem related to the size of their breasts.
A medical professional, such as a plastic surgeon or a dermatologist, can assess whether breast reduction surgery is a suitable option based on an individual's specific symptoms and medical history.

Ideal candidates have gigantomastia: excessively large breasts, back pain, sagging breasts, and poor posture. Also, those who have had very large implants and need a reduction. If you recognize yourself in these symptoms, this corrective surgery will improve your health and quality of life.

Ideal if breast size affects your health and your daily life

* Gigantomastia and hypertrophy
* Back pain and poor posture
* Implants that are too large

3 techniques

ϟ

Techniques: periareolar, vertical, or inverted T

In smaller volumes, the incision is periareolar (around the areola). The vertical technique adds an incision down to the inframammary fold. For larger, flaccid, or sagging breasts, the inverted T or anchor technique is used, with three incisions that allow for the removal of excess tissue, breast lift, and symmetry.

The technique is chosen according to the volume and the degree of fall

* Periareolar (smaller volumes)
* Vertical
* Inverted T or anchor

∿

How is the intervention carried out?

During the surgery, the areola and nipple are removed to excise excess breast tissue and skin; the areola is reduced in size if necessary before being repositioned. The result: smaller, lifted, and more proportionate breasts that fit your body.

Areola-nipple complex repositioning included

* Removal of excess tissue
* Breast lift
* Symmetry and proportion

◎

General anesthesia and 2-3 hours

The surgery is performed under general anesthesia: you will be asleep for the entire procedure. The surgical time ranges from 2 to 3 hours, and in very complex cases, it can take up to 4 hours. In some cases, it is combined with a breast lift during the same surgery.

Can be combined with a breast lift in the same procedure

* General anesthesia
* 2-3 hours (up to 4 for complex ones)
* Combinable with lift

## How to Prepare for Your Breast Reduction: Step-by-Step

We guide you step-by-step to ensure your breast surgery is completely calm and safe.

In the first consultation, the surgeon studies your case in detail: breast volume and shape, back posture, position and size of the areola-nipple complex, and skin texture and flexibility. With this information, they define the appropriate surgical technique and inform you of the results and potential complications.

Pre-testing: laboratory analysis, follow-up ultrasounds and mammography, plus electrocardiogram and chest x-ray ordered by the internist and anesthesiologist. Days prior: balanced diet, suspend medications that interfere with coagulation, fast for 8 hours, and refrain from alcohol and tobacco at least 4 weeks beforehand.

The intervention is performed under general anesthesia and lasts between 2 and 3 hours (up to 4 in complex cases). Personalized surgical marking and a final interview with the anesthesiologist before entering the operating room.

## Risks of Breast Reduction: What You Should Know

As with any surgical procedure, breast reduction involves risks that are important to be aware of, although they are not common thanks to the thorough preoperative assessment before making a decision.

Common effects after reduction

They are expected to improve in the first few weeks with the prescribed treatments.

* Temporary alteration of sensation
* Inflammation and bruising in the first few days
* Reddish scars at first that fade
* Manageable discomfort with analgesia

Rare with good preoperative condition

The prior study and the hospital environment make them exceptional.

* Abnormal scarring
* Difficulties breastfeeding in the future
* Hemorrhages or seromas
* Infections or allergic reactions

## Frequently Asked Questions about Breast Reduction Surgery in Barcelona

Resolve your doubts about breast reduction surgery

## The technique

### What is breast reduction surgery?

This is the surgery that corrects mammary hypertrophy: it removes excess fat and tissue, lifts the breasts' position, and, if necessary, reduces the size of the areola. The result is smaller, symmetrical breasts that are in harmony with your body.

### How many sizes can the breast be reduced?

Generally, two or three sizes, although it varies for each patient. The goal is to correct volume and asymmetry by adapting the breast to your body proportions; the final bra size will correspond to the result.

### What techniques exist?

Three, depending on the case: periareolar (smaller volumes), vertical (incision around the areola and down to the fold), and inverted T or anchor, for larger or sagging breasts, which also allows for breast lift. The technique is determined during the pre-consultation.

## The process

### What is the price of breast reduction surgery in Barcelona?

In Spain, it usually ranges from €4,500 to €9,500, with most standard cases between €5,500 and €6,500. The budget includes surgeon's fees, anesthesia, operating room or hospitalization costs, and follow-up check-ups. It varies depending on the technique, complexity, and medical team.

### Does social security cover breast reduction surgery?

Only cases of gigantomastia that pose a health risk: breasts with dimensions of about 60 centimeters and a minimum bra size of 105/110. Each autonomous community assigns a protocol, usually through the primary care physician who refers the case to the surgeon, or through psychological evaluation due to affectation of mental health.

### What anesthesia is used and how long does the surgery last?

General anesthesia: You will remain asleep for the entire procedure. The surgical time ranges from 2 to 3 hours, and in very complex cases, it can be up to 4 hours.

## Recovery

### What is the postoperative period like?

After surgery, a dressing is applied, and if necessary, a drain, which is removed during the first week's follow-up appointment along with the dressing changes. The dressing is replaced with a special bra. At 4 weeks, you can walk and engage in light activity; strenuous exertion should wait at least 6 weeks.

### What precautions should be taken?

Complete the prescribed antibiotics, do not moisten the bandage, rest, avoid direct sun, do not lift weights or raise your arms above your shoulders. The surgeon may recommend antibiotic creams on the wounds and moisturizers on the surrounding skin.

### What is a good time to have surgery?

The cool months (spring and autumn) are recommended to avoid healing problems associated with heat. The ideal time is when you have time to recover and the surgeon confirms you are ready.

## Results and scars

### What scars does breast reduction surgery leave?

According to the technique, around the areola or in an anchor shape (areola, vertical, and inframammary fold). Initially, they appear reddish and rough, but over time they flatten and blend with the skin tone.

### Is sensation lost?

It's a latent risk, especially in very large breasts: it can affect the areola, nipple, or skin near the scars. It's not permanent; sensitivity gradually returns.

### What benefits will I notice?

Relief from back pain, improved posture, disappearance of irritation and infections in the mammary fold and shoulder grooves from the bra. And on a personal level: comfort, confidence, and a noticeable increase in self-esteem.
