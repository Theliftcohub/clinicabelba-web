# Dr. Mike Dewever

Inició sus estudios en la Université Libre de Bruxelles y terminó licenciándose en la Universidad de Barcelona y recibiendo el premio extraordinario de licenciatura por sus brillantes resultados académicos. Fue el número 75 del examen MIR, de entre mas de 13.000 participantes. Este logro le permitió realizar su residencia en el Hospital Universitario de Bellvitge. Allí se especializó en cirugía mamaria, plástica y reconstructiva. Al acabar la residencia fue contratado como médico adjunto del servicio de cirugía plástica del mismo Hospital Universitario de Bellvitge. Completó su formación como [Plastic surgeon in Barcelona](https://clinicabelba.com/en/plastic-surgeons-barcelona/) in the **Teknon Clinic**, and at Columbia University, New York. He is the author and co-author of several national and international publications. He is a member of Secpre and SCCP. His expertise is recognized at the European level with the title of Fellow of the European Board of Plastic Surgery (EBOPRAS 2016).

Since 2017, he has dedicated himself exclusively to private surgery, with very successful results, as the satisfaction of his patients attests to the excellence of his work.

He has participated in several courses with him **Dr. Alfredo Hoyos,** world leader in body surgery. In breast surgery, he is a pioneer in minimally invasive breast surgery, with rapid recovery and natural results, performing **Masterclass on his minimal scar technique.**

He has worked for 4 years at the prestigious **Planas Clinic**, where he has performed over 600 surgeries, his accumulated experience represents over 3000 surgeries.

### Body Surgery

**Tummy tuck**
The [Tummy tuck](https://clinicabelba.com/en/tummy-tuck-barcelona/) It is a surgery intended to improve the abdomen, removing excess skin and fatty tissue to achieve a flat, firm belly with a defined waist. To correct weakness or separation of the abdominal muscles (diastasis recti), a plication of the rectus muscles is performed. To improve and define the waist, liposuction of the flanks is performed. The patient will regain the firmness and rigidity of a flat stomach. Additionally, her self-esteem will be boosted with a defined waist that will beautify her silhouette.

**Liposuction**
The [liposuction](https://clinicabelba.com/en/liposuction-barcelona/) It involves suctioning excess localized fat from a specific area of the body. It is a surgery indicated for patients who are trying to reduce fat in a localized area and cannot get rid of it through exercise or diet. It is important to understand that liposuction is not indicated for weight loss.

Liposculpture involves performing liposuction while preserving the extracted fat to process it and inject it into another area of the body, such as the breasts, buttocks, or face.

### Facial Surgery

**Ultrasonic Rhinoplasty**
The anatomy of the nose is very complex and varied, so a nose surgery cannot be generalized, as each patient requires a diagnosis and an individualized surgical plan. The most common requests are to correct deviations and size, remove humps, or refine the tip of the nose.

Technology allows for a much faster, less painful, and less invasive recovery than conventional techniques.

**Blepharoplasty**
The [Blepharoplasty](https://clinicabelba.com/en/blepharoplasty/) It is a surgery intended to enhance the patient's gaze, providing a younger and more intense appearance. Upper and lower eyelid surgery can be combined, or performed separately, depending on each patient's needs.

**Lifting:**
Each face ages differently, which is why personalized consultation is necessary according to each patient's needs to achieve optimal results. The goal is to restore the vitality of a youthful face and skin firmness.

**Otoplasty**
[Otoplasty](https://clinicabelba.com/en/otoplasty/) is the name given to the ear reshaping surgery. These are a key feature that gives the face harmony; large, very protruding, or irregularly sized ears create an unbalanced appearance, and this is precisely what this cosmetic surgery corrects.

### Genital Surgery

**Nymphectomy**
Nymphoplasty is a reduction surgery that corrects hypertrophy of the labia minora. It is performed not just for aesthetic reasons, but also because the surgery is necessary for functional reasons, as hypertrophy can cause discomfort during sexual relations, when dressing, playing sports, or simply when sitting. For these reasons, the [Labiaplasty in Barcelona](https://clinicabelba.com/en/labiaplasty/) It is a surgery that is performed more and more frequently.
