# Types of breast augmentation

## Subtle Increase

This type of augmentation focuses on enhancing the natural beauty of the breasts in a discreet and elegant way. It provides a subtle increase in volume that improves the shape and proportion of the breasts, maintaining a natural and harmonious appearance. With «Subtle Augmentation,» the goal is a delicate yet noticeable impact that enhances the patient's femininity in a refined manner.

## Harmony Increase

The “Harmony Augmentation” seeks to achieve perfect proportion and symmetry between the patient's breasts and body. It provides a volume increase that integrates harmoniously with the overall figure, creating a balanced and aesthetically pleasing appearance. With this type of augmentation, the goal is an impact that enhances the patient's natural beauty in a proportionate and elegant manner.

## Increase Elegance

The «Elegance Enhancement» combines naturalness with a touch of sophistication and refinement. It provides increased volume that enhances the shape and projection of the breasts, creating an elegant and distinguished appearance. With this type of enhancement, the goal is to achieve an impact that elegantly and sophisticatedly reflects the patient's natural beauty.

With this type of augmentation, the goal is an impact that brilliantly and captivatingly reflects the patient's natural beauty.

The «Magnificent Augmentation» is distinguished by its grandeur and splendor. It provides a generous volume increase, creating an impressive and majestic appearance. With this type of augmentation, the goal is an impact that magnificently and dazzlingly highlights the patient's femininity, providing spectacular and long-lasting results.
