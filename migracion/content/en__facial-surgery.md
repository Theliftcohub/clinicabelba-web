## What is facial plastic surgery?

Facial plastic surgery encompasses surgical procedures aimed at improving the aesthetics, structure, and proportion of the face. It can help treat signs of aging, excess skin, sagging tissues, eyelid bags, nasal deformities, loss of jawline definition, prominent ears, altered earlobes, or facial disharmony.

Unlike facial aesthetic medicine treatments such as Botox, hyaluronic acid, and biostimulation, facial plastic surgery acts on deeper structures and allows for the correction of anatomical changes that do not respond to non-surgical treatments.

Every face is different. Planning must be personalized and carried out by a medical team specialized in facial surgery.

Treatments

## Types of facial surgery at Belba Clinic

Breast surgery is not a single procedure. It is a set of surgical interventions — each with its own specific indication, technique, and recovery process — that our plastic surgeons master with the same level of expertise. Whether you are looking for breast augmentation, reduction, lift, or correction of a previous surgery, the starting point is always the same: an individualized medical evaluation where your surgeon assesses your case, explains your options, and honestly tells you what makes the most sense for you.

## Facelift

Facelifts are indicated for patients with significant sagging, tissue ptosis, or loss of definition in the face and neck. Their goal is to reposition facial tissues and achieve a more rejuvenated and fresh appearance without altering natural expression. It is the most impactful facial surgery procedure for overall aging of the mid and lower thirds of the face and neck. [**Facelift**](https://clinicabelba.com/en/facelift-barcelona/)

## Deep plane facelift

The **[Deep plane facelift](https://clinicabelba.com/lifting-denise-richards/)** It is an advanced facial rejuvenation technique that works on deeper planes than conventional facelifts. It allows for the repositioning of tissues in a more natural and lasting way, avoiding the tight appearance associated with more superficial techniques. It is indicated for more pronounced aging or when a more complete and lasting correction is sought. [**Facelift**](https://clinicabelba.com/en/facelift-barcelona/)

## Mini facelift

A mini facelift may be indicated for patients with mild to moderate sagging who are seeking to improve facial definition with a less extensive approach than a full facelift. The surgical time and recovery are shorter, although the results are also more limited—something our surgeons explain clearly during the consultation. [**Mini facelift**](https://clinicabelba.com/en/mini-lifting-facial-barcelona/)

## Blepharoplasty

The [**Blepharoplasty**](https://clinicabelba.com/en/blepharoplasty/) It allows treating excess skin on the upper eyelids, bags under the lower eyelids, or a tired look that doesn't reflect how you feel. It is one of the most requested facial surgery procedures due to its direct impact on the expression and freshness of the face — with a relatively quick recovery compared to other facial procedures. [**Blepharoplasty**](https://clinicabelba.com/en/blepharoplasty/)

## Rhinoplasty

La rinoplastia permite modificar la forma, tamaño o proyección de la nariz para mejorar su proporción con el resto del rostro. Puede tener un objetivo estético, funcional o ambos. Es uno de los procedimientos de cirugía plástica facial más complejos, porque pequeños cambios en la nariz tienen un impacto muy visible en la armonía global del rostro. [**View rhinoplasty**](https://clinicabelba.com/en/rhinoplasty-barcelona/)

## Nose remodeling

Nose remodeling is a non-surgical aesthetic medicine treatment that allows for the improvement of small details of the nose through injections, without going under the knife. It can correct minor irregularities or improve the nasal profile in selected patients seeking a subtle change. It does not replace rhinoplasty when significant nasal structure modification, volume reduction, or functional problems need to be addressed. [**→ See rinomodeling**](https://clinicabelba.com/en/rhinomodeling-barcelona/)

## Otoplasty

Otoplasty corrects prominent or protruding ears, improving facial proportion and symmetry. It is a facial surgery with a real impact on patient confidence and a relatively comfortable recovery. It can be performed from adolescence onwards. [**→ See otoplasty**](https://clinicabelba.com/en/otoplasty/)

## Lobuloplasty

Lobuloplasty allows for the correction of torn, elongated, or deformed earlobes — due to heavy earrings, accidental tears, or simply the passage of time. It is an outpatient minor facial surgery procedure, performed under local anesthesia with a very quick recovery, which has a more significant aesthetic impact than might appear at first glance. [**→ See lobuloplasty**](https://clinicabelba.com/en/lobuloplasty-barcelona/)

## Facial fat grafting

Facial fat grafting uses the patient's own fat to restore volume in areas of the face that have lost it over time: cheekbones, nasolabial folds, under-eye hollows, or areas with loss of support. The result is natural because it's the patient's own tissue, and it has the advantage of combining facial rejuvenation with discreet reshaping of the donor area. **Check availability for medical assessment**

## Chin surgery

Mentoplasty allows you to improve chin definition, facial profile balance, or the harmony between the jaw, nose, and other facial structures. It's a procedure with a significant impact on the overall proportion of the face — especially in profiles where the chin is retracted or not well-defined. [**→ See mentoplasty**](https://clinicabelba.com/en/mentoplasty-barcelona/)

Risks

## What you should know before your facelift surgery: risks and preoperative information

At Clínica Belba, we believe medical transparency is part of care. Like any surgical intervention, facial surgery carries risks that are important to know before making a decision—and our plastic surgeons explain them to you in detail during the pre-operative consultation. Here, we give you an overview.

**Most frequent** *(they resolve themselves during the first few months in the vast majority of cases)*

* Inflammation and bruising in the operated area
* Temporary changes in skin sensitivity on the face or neck
* Transient asymmetry during recovery
* Tightness or a feeling of stiffness in the first few weeks after a facelift

**Less common**

* Temporary alteration of facial mobility. Uncommon with an expert plastic surgeon and appropriate technique.
* Hypertrophic or keloid scar, depending on individual predisposition
* Residual asymmetry, controllable with correct postoperative follow-up
* Infection, treatable with the corresponding medical protocol.

## Frequently Asked Questions About Facial Plastic Surgery

Resolve your most common questions about breast augmentation surgery

### What is facial plastic surgery?

Facial plastic surgery encompasses surgical procedures aimed at improving the harmony, structure, or rejuvenation of the face: [**Facelift**](https://clinicabelba.com/en/facelift-barcelona/), [**Blepharoplasty**](https://clinicabelba.com/en/blepharoplasty/), [**rhinoplasty**](https://clinicabelba.com/en/rhinoplasty-barcelona/), **[Otoplasty](https://clinicabelba.com/en/otoplasty/)**, [**lobuloplasty,**](https://clinicabelba.com/en/lobuloplasty-barcelona/) [**Facial lipofilling and mentoplasty.**](https://clinicabelba.com/en/mentoplasty-barcelona/) It acts on deeper layers than aesthetic medicine and offers more durable and structural results.

### ¿Cuál es la cirugía plástica facial más demandada?

Among the most in-demand in Spain are the [**Blepharoplasty**](https://clinicabelba.com/en/facelift-barcelona/), the [**rhinoplasty**](https://clinicabelba.com/en/rhinoplasty-barcelona/) and the **[Facelift](https://clinicabelba.com/en/facelift-barcelona/)**. The choice always depends on the area to be treated and the individual medical assessment.

### What is the difference between a facelift and a deep plane facelift?

The conventional facelift works on the more superficial layers of tissue. The deep plane facelift works on deeper planes, allowing for more complete repositioning of tissues with a more natural and lasting result. It is indicated for more pronounced aging. You can see more on our page **[Facelift](https://clinicabelba.com/en/facelift-barcelona/)**

### Is there a facelift without surgery?

There are non-surgical treatments that tighten the skin superficially, such as threads, ultrasound, and radiofrequency, which in some contexts are referred to as «non-surgical facelifts.» Their results are more limited and temporary than those of a surgical facelift, especially in cases of significant sagging. Our surgeons will honestly explain which option makes the most sense for your case.

### What results can I expect from facial surgery?

The results of a well-planned facial surgery are natural, harmonious, and long-lasting. Swelling takes several weeks to subside, and the final result can be seen between 3 and 6 months after the procedure.

### When to consider a facelift?

When there is sagging of the face or neck, loss of jawline definition, tissue ptosis, or visible signs of aging that do not improve with non-surgical treatments. More information on the page **[Facelift](https://clinicabelba.com/en/facelift-barcelona/)**

### Does facial surgery change your expression?

A well-planned facial surgery should respect the patient's natural expression. The goal is to rejuvenate or harmonize the face, not to alter their identity.

### How much does facial surgery cost in Spain?

The price depends on the procedure, the complexity of the case, the technique, and the included follow-up. The medical evaluation allows for a personalized and transparent budget.
