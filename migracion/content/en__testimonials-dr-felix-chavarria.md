***Ana tells us about her experience:***

*Hello, my name is Ana.
I had a breast lift and a mini tummy tuck with rectus plication, and I am very happy with the result.
My biggest fear was anesthesia, but I knew it was unlikely that anything serious would happen.
My main concern was that there would be unsightly scars, but I am very happy because after a little over two months, they are barely noticeable.
The breast recovery was excellent, with no discomfort, just a little pressure. The rectus plication recovery was a bit more difficult, but within a week I was back to my normal life.
I met Dr. Chavarría on Dr. Antonio Machado's recommendation, and I am very happy with my decision.
Without a doubt, I recommend it.*
