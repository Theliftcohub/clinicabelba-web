## Preserved Breast Augmentation Techniques: Which One is Yours?

Preserved breast augmentation is not a single technique applied the same way to all patients. Its planning depends on breast anatomy, desired volume, skin quality, tissue elasticity, and the type of implant selected.

The goal is to achieve a natural, stable, and proportionate breast augmentation, while respecting the breast structure as much as possible.

♡

Breast Augmentation Preserved with Motiva implants

Breast Augmentation Preserved is usually associated with Motiva implants, designed to integrate harmoniously with the tissues and promote a natural outcome. This option aims to increase breast volume without straining the anatomy, prioritizing the progressive adaptation of the tissues to the implant.

Ideal for patients who desire natural breast augmentation, with state-of-the-art implants and a gentler recovery.

Preserved technique with an expandable balloon

Ideal for patients seeking a less invasive technique with reduced inflammation and greater respect for anatomy.

## How to Prepare for Your Breast Augmentation

The preparation before a Breast Augmentation Preserved is fundamental to define a safe, personalized technique that is consistent with the result each patient seeks.

**Personalized breast evaluation**

In the initial consultation, the breast shape, current volume, skin elasticity, areola position, symmetry, and the relationship between the chest and the breast are analyzed. This assessment allows us to determine if the patient is a candidate for the Preservé technique and what type of implant can best integrate with her tissues.

**Include:**

* Study of mammary anatomy
* Fabric Quality Assessment
* Expectations analysis
* Personalized implant selection

**Implant Selection and Surgical Planning**

The choice of implant depends not only on the desired size. The width of the chest, the breast base, projection, skin elasticity, and the patient's lifestyle are also considered. At Preservé, the implant must adapt to the anatomy without straining the tissues.

**Include:**

* Volume selection
* Profile Selection and Projection

Before surgery, medical tests may be requested to confirm that the patient is in adequate condition for the procedure.

Recovery Start

During the first few days, swelling, tightness, sensitivity, and discomfort may appear, which can be controlled with the prescribed medication. It is important to rest, avoid exertion, and follow the medical team's instructions.

* Relative rest
* Use of post-operative bra if indicated
* Sleeping on your back
* Avoid lifting heavy weights
* Do not raise your arms suddenly
* Attend the first check-up

Care and warning signs

Inflammation begins to improve, and many patients can resume light activities, as long as they do not involve physical exertion. Recovery may vary depending on anatomy, implant, and individual response.

* Keep the indicated bra
* Avoid sports
* Do not drive until medically cleared.
* Monitor for redness, fever, or intense pain
* Follow the healing and medication guidelines

Activity, healing, and evolution

In this phase, the breast continues to adapt to the implant, and swelling continues to decrease. More normal daily activities can be resumed, although high-impact exercises, heavy lifting, or intense upper body movements should still be avoided.

* Progressive activity
* Medical reviews
* Scar care
* Avoid straining your arms and chest.
* Follow instructions about massages if prescribed

Final results

Starting from the sixth week, many patients can move towards a more complete recovery, always with medical authorization. The breast continues to settle in the following months, and the scars continue their maturation process.

* Resume deporte según indicación
* Protect scars from the sun
* Keep scheduled reviews
* Evaluate implant evolution

## Risks of Breast Augmentation Preservation: What You Need to Know

Although breast augmentation preservation is a less invasive technique and aims to preserve tissue, it is still a surgical procedure. Therefore, it is important to be aware of its potential risks, resolve any doubts before surgery, and follow medical instructions correctly.

Common side effects during recovery

During the first few days or weeks, normal postoperative discomfort may appear.

* Inflammation
* Tie rod
* Breast tenderness
* Mild hematomas
* Temporary changes in sensitivity
* Transient asymmetry during adaptation

Less frequent risks

As with any breast surgery with implants, there are less common risks that must be explained before the procedure.

* Infection
* Bleeding or bruising
* Seroma
* Poor healing
* Capsular contracture
* Implant displacement
* Need for surgical revision

## Frequently Asked Questions about Preserved Breast Augmentation

Resolve your most common questions about breast augmentation surgery

## The technique

### Is Preservé breast augmentation more natural?

Yes. By respecting the native tissues and the natural attachment of the breast, the result can look and feel more natural. The goal is for the implant to adapt harmoniously to the patient's anatomy.

### Is PreservÉ a less invasive technique?

Yes. Compared to traditional techniques, Preservé seeks to minimize damage to breast structures and promote a more gradual adaptation of the tissue to the implant.

### Can it be combined with Motiva implants?

Yes. The Preservé technique is frequently associated with Motiva implants, which are designed to better integrate with this conservative approach.

## The process

### Who is a candidate for a breast augmentation?

A patient seeking a natural result, who values a less invasive technique, and has suitable anatomy for this type of approach can be a candidate. The indication must always be made after a personalized medical evaluation.

### Does Preserve work for any breast size?

Not necessarily. The technique must be adapted to the anatomy, tissues, and desired volume. If a very large increase is sought or there is significant sagging, it may be necessary to consider other techniques or combinations.

### How much does the Preservé breast augmentation cost in Barcelona?

The Preservé technique typically costs approximately between €6,990 and €8,500 in Spain, including the operating room, medical team, prosthesis, and post-operative follow-up, though the final quote must be personalized after a medical evaluation.

## Recovery

### Is recovery faster than with a conventional breast augmentation?

In many cases, as it is a less invasive technique, recovery can be more comfortable and with less inflammation. However, each patient progresses differently and must follow the medical team's instructions.

### What type of bra is used post-surgery?

After surgery, a post-surgical or compression bra is usually recommended. It's designed to support the breasts, reduce swelling, and promote proper tissue adaptation during the initial weeks. The exact type of bra and duration of use depend on each individual case and the surgeon's instructions.

### Do the results last longer over time?

Anatomical preservation can favor greater stability and more harmonious long-term evolution. Nevertheless, the duration of the result will depend on factors such as skin, natural aging, weight changes, and medical check-ups.

## Results and scars

### How does Preservé breast augmentation differ from traditional augmentation?

In traditional augmentation, tissues may be stretched more to create space for the implant. In Preservé, the aim is to preserve breast anatomy and adapt tissues in a more controlled manner, reducing surgical aggression.

### Does this technique leave visible scars?

All surgery leaves a scar, but at Preservé the incision can be reduced and located in a discreet area.

### Can preservation be combined with a breast lift?

Yes, in some cases it can be combined with a breast lift if, in addition to a lack of volume, there is breast sagging. The indication will depend on the degree of ptosis, the position of the nipple, and the quality of the skin.
