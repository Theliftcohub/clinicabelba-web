## What is abdominoplasty?

The **Tummy tuck**, also called **tummy tuck**, es una cirugía plástica que combina la eliminación del exceso de piel y grasa del abdomen con la tonificación de la musculatura de la pared abdominal, logrando un vientre más plano y una cintura más definida. En muchos casos se aborda también la separación de los músculos rectos o la flacidez tras embarazos o pérdidas importantes de peso.

It is indicated in patients with **excess abdominal skin**, **marked flaccidity** o **Muscle wall weakness**. During the medical evaluation, the surgeon determines if this surgery is the right option, if it's advisable to combine it with a [liposuction](https://clinicabelba.com/en/liposuction-barcelona/) or if there are less invasive alternatives for your case.

The goal isn't to artificially transform the body, but to reshape the area to improve figure, comfort, and body confidence.

## Who is a good candidate for a tummy tuck in Barcelona?

Indicated in patients with **stable weight**, **Good general health** y **Realistic expectations** — women and men who:

✓They present persistent abdominal sagging and excess skin

✓They have just stabilized their weight after significant losses.

✓They have gone through pregnancies with abdominal dilation

✓They show diastasis or weakness of the muscle wall

✓They are looking for a flat and proportionate abdomen.

✓They maintain healthy lifestyles

Warning**Important:** It is not a weight loss method. It is not recommended if future pregnancies or significant short-term weight changes are planned. The surgeon evaluates each case individually during the first consultation.

How to do it: step-by-step procedure

From Consultation to Recovery: Complete Medical Support

01

Initial Assessment and Planning

Before surgery, we perform a complete evaluation of your abdomen, review your medical history, and define a personalized plan. During this visit, we determine if you need a mini-abdominoplasty, a full abdominoplasty, or a combined technique with liposuction.

02

Pre-operative marking and anesthesia

The surgeon performs precise markings on the abdomen to define the areas to be corrected, the amount of skin to be removed, and the final design of the navel if necessary. The procedure is performed under general anesthesia to ensure your comfort and safety.

03

The intervention: incision, muscle, and skin

An incision is made low down, just above the pubic bone, designed to be hidden by underwear or a bikini (in a full tummy tuck, also around the navel). If there is diastasis, the abdominal muscles are tightened and reinforced; then excess skin and fat are removed, reshaping the abdomen.

04

Closure, band, and immediate postoperative period

The tissues are tightened, the incision is closed with fine sutures, and bandages or a compression garment are applied. You will remain under observation and, depending on your case, will be discharged the same day or after an overnight stay, with clear instructions on mobility, care, and scheduled follow-ups.

## What you need to know before your tummy tuck: risks and pre-operative information

At Clínica Belba, we believe medical transparency is part of care. Like any surgical procedure, abdominoplasty carries risks that are important to know before making a decision—and our plastic surgeons will explain them to you in detail during your pre-operative consultation.

**Most frequent** *(they resolve themselves during the first few months in the vast majority of cases)*

* Swelling and bruising in the abdomen the first few days
* Tightness and temporary changes in sensitivity
* Manageable discomfort with scheduled analgesia
* Seroma - fluid accumulation, controllable with medical monitoring

**Less common**

* Skin fibrosis or irregularities, reduced with proper technique and correct use of a compression garment
* Infection, treatable with the corresponding medical protocol.
* Deep vein thrombosis — low risk, reduced with standard preventive measures
* Hypertrophic or keloid scar, depending on the individual predisposition of each patient

Frequently Asked Questions

## Frequently Asked Questions about Abdominoplasty

Solve your most common tummy tuck questions

### Is abdominoplasty painful?

The postoperative period usually involves manageable discomfort and an initial feeling of tightness. We provide scheduled pain relief and recommendations to make your recovery as comfortable as possible.

### Are there any visible scars?

The incision is placed low on the abdomen, designed to be hidden by underwear or a bikini. With proper care, it gradually fades.

### When will I see the final result?

The improvement is visible from the first few weeks. The result stabilizes as the inflammation subsides and the tissues adapt; the final assessment is usually made between 3 and 6 months.

### Can I combine a tummy tuck with liposuction?

Yes, in selected cases: it helps to refine the flanks and waist (lipoabdominoplasty). It is indicated when it provides a clear benefit to the overall contour and safety. It can also be combined with an arm lift.

### Who should not have surgery?

Individuals with uncontrolled illnesses or unrealistic expectations. We also recommend postponing surgery if short-term pregnancies are planned, to preserve the outcome.

### When can I return to work and exercise?

The return to work typically occurs between two and four weeks, depending on the type of activity. Exercise is reintroduced gradually and progressively.

### Abdominoplasty removes stretch marks.

You can improve your appearance if the skin that is removed during surgery is affected. Not all stretch marks disappear; each case is evaluated individually.

### Are the results final?

Yes, as long as you maintain a healthy lifestyle and a stable weight. Significant weight changes can affect the outcome.

### How much does a tummy tuck cost in Barcelona?

Between €4,500 and €8,000, with an average price of €6,000-€6,500. It depends on whether it's a full or mini treatment, whether it's combined with liposuction, and the included follow-up. The final quote is determined after the medical evaluation.

### Who is a good candidate?

Patients with stable weight, good health, and realistic expectations, who have excess skin, sagging, or diastasis after pregnancies or weight loss. Not recommended if future pregnancies are planned in the short term.
