## Plastic surgery

![rhinoplasty](https://clinicabelba.com/wp-content/uploads/elementor/thumbs/descarga-17-rkehofpl6twurno1fq6v9lhmzz1y8i4or93dljvz4w.png "rhinoplasty")

Plastic surgery aims to provide harmony and well-being to patients with signs of aging or deformities, restoring self-esteem and promoting the individual's reintegration into their social group. The specialty has gained enormous importance in modern society, as a good appearance can exert a significant influence in various areas of social, professional, and even personal life. When we improve our appearance, we increase our pride and self-esteem—this maximizes our actions in all aspects. In short, the philosophy of plastic surgery is to help the patient improve their quality of life.

![Facial lift in Barcelona](https://clinicabelba.com/wp-content/uploads/elementor/thumbs/descarga-18-e1679311708516-rkehop3z369pzradwu94yj48xtrmdh604jm8ebi1eo.png "Facial lift in Barcelona")

Many ask what the reasons for plastic surgery would be. The answer is to try to fulfill life's fundamental purpose: to achieve a sense of fulfillment and happiness. However, the patient seeking plastic surgery must understand that the treatment is not capable of solving problems such as marital unhappiness, professional difficulties, and psychiatric disorders, especially if the patient attributes these problems to their appearance. Therefore, the motives should always be realistic.

![Best eyelid surgery surgeon Barcelona](https://clinicabelba.com/wp-content/uploads/elementor/thumbs/descarga-19-rkehoyicziml7uwqdybengquvohaig7bhu537343og.png "Best eyelid surgery surgeon Barcelona")

La cirugía plástica fue desarrollada buscando revertir o disfrazar algunas señales evidentes de envejecimiento. Sin embargo, no siempre es posible recrear las condiciones que existían en la juventud, además de que a veces es imposible alcanzar las expectativas del paciente. Por ende, a través de cirugías cuidadosamente planificadas, cuya técnica es perfeccionada luego de muchos años de arduo entrenamiento, experiencia, conocimiento y habilidad quirúrgica, el objetivo del cirujano es mejorar la apariencia de su paciente de la manera más natural y equilibrada posible.

![Otoplasty Barcelona](https://clinicabelba.com/wp-content/uploads/elementor/thumbs/descarga-20-rkehqg35t2nxlmqwp3h53l84qn57p740p7brjuwnts.png "otoplasty Barcelona")

It is important to realize that plastic surgery is not an exact science, but rather a specialty that involves a high degree of artistic subjectivity. Furthermore, the surgeon is accustomed to being challenged by various options during the planning and execution of a surgery. Therefore, it is important to be aware that the degree of success of a surgery depends on several factors, and not just the doctor's skill. Aspects such as age, health, skin texture, tissue quality, bone structure, associated diseases, patient expectations, and inherent healing factors decisively influence the outcome. When all these variables collaborate, the chances of obtaining a satisfactory result increase significantly...

El boom de la cirugía plástica tornó difícil identificar a un cirujano plástico calificado. En tanto antes la cirugía plástica era realizada por cirujanos plásticos, en la actualidad muchos otros médicos ofrecen procedimientos estéticos. La búsqueda para encontrar a un cirujano plástico calificado es solo uno de los retos que enfrentará. Una planificación cuidadosa y un abordaje organizado son esenciales cuando se persigue un resultado satisfactorio. No economice tiempo, ni dinero, ni disposición. Muchos pacientes emplean más tiempo y dinero haciendo compras que escogiendo a su médico. Cuidado: es su cuerpo—¡lo verá por el resto de su vida!

![download-19.png](https://clinicabelba.com/wp-content/uploads/elementor/thumbs/descarga-19-rkeiat99vujl5h5xrwe54g7o36rih3yni06hvopl0g.png "download-19.png")

**How to choose a doctor**

The plastic surgeon's training is the most influential factor in the quality of a plastic surgery outcome. Basic training includes 6 years of medical school, 2 years of general surgery, and 3 years of plastic surgery residency. Some even pursue a subspecialization for 1 or 2 years, reaching the highest level of specialization a doctor can achieve in a specific surgery. Therefore, seek a doctor trained at a recognized institution—ask about their specializations, experience, whether they teach and take courses, if they have participated in publications, and who they have worked with. In short, interview them as if you were an HR professional looking for the best candidate. Look for a specialist in the desired surgery; that is, if you intend to have a nose job, ensure they are a nose specialist.

![download-20.png](https://clinicabelba.com/wp-content/uploads/elementor/thumbs/descarga-20-rkeib7cuqd2vzmlghkhjnunkzyu0okimjxys2u4of4.png "download-20.png")

Following the directions of people who have had surgery from that doctor is a good way to make the right choice of professional. In addition to verifying the results, ask the person about all stages of the treatment, how the doctor behaved before and after the surgery. People who work at the hospital, namely nurses, receptionists, as well as other doctors, are excellent sources, as they observe the surgeon's work in their daily tasks, as well as their behavior in the operating room and with patients…

## Plastic surgery anesthesia

![download-19.png](https://clinicabelba.com/wp-content/uploads/elementor/thumbs/descarga-19-1-rkeic1fot282b5drlxhlvn2c0aprivu1c2ubfow2w0.png "download-19.png")

**Introduction**

Anesthesia is a fundamental procedure in maintaining patient health during surgery. It must be performed by a medical professional anesthesiologist, a member of their state's society. The anesthesiologist must possess practical experience with all procedures, emergency resolution, and must be certified in the proper operation of equipment, offering the patient surgery with maximum safety levels.

![download-18.png](https://clinicabelba.com/wp-content/uploads/elementor/thumbs/descarga-18-1-rkeibr3gpvtwrfssab0pm7o9h24q67ozmnnz5nbesg.png "download-18.png")

**The pre-anesthetic visit**

The pre-anesthetic visit is the first contact with the anesthesiologist. In this brief interview, the patient's clinical and surgical history, as well as any illnesses and current medications, are discussed in detail. The anesthesiologist must review the preoperative tests, explain the stages of anesthesia, and detail the typical reactions during awakening. At the end of the interview, the patient (who must have been fasting for at least 8 hours) receives a pre-anesthetic medication to induce sleep and reduce memory of the initial maneuvers of anesthesia in the operating room, making the patient more comfortable.

![download-19.png](https://clinicabelba.com/wp-content/uploads/elementor/thumbs/descarga-19-1-rkeic1fot282b5drlxhlvn2c0aprivu1c2ubfow2w0.png "download-19.png")

**The operating room**

The operating room must have current and reliable equipment, being comfortable for both the patient and the medical team. Of the necessary equipment, the cardiac monitor, blood pressure monitor, digital oximetry, and warming systems are now the minimum monitoring required for any surgical procedure. These devices must be checked daily for the surgery to be performed safely.

![download-20.png](https://clinicabelba.com/wp-content/uploads/elementor/thumbs/descarga-20-1-rkeichey18txsiqk0me9k1163uj05qlh29xkle8dy8.png "download-20.png")

**Types of anesthesia**

There are three main types of anesthesia in plastic surgery: combined local (local combined with sedation), regional anesthesia, and general anesthesia. Combined local anesthesia can be used in various procedures, from minor ones, which are the most frequent, to more prolonged surgeries. This technique is preferably used for patients in good health and who are emotionally and physically stable…

## Make the decision

![download-20.png](https://clinicabelba.com/wp-content/uploads/elementor/thumbs/descarga-20-1-rkeichey18txsiqk0me9k1163uj05qlh29xkle8dy8.png "download-20.png")

* Los principales factores que motivan al paciente a buscar una cirugía plástica.

![download-18.png](https://clinicabelba.com/wp-content/uploads/elementor/thumbs/descarga-18-1-rkeibr3gpvtwrfssab0pm7o9h24q67ozmnnz5nbesg.png "download-18.png")

Actualmente, personas de diversas edades, profesiones distintas y todas las clases sociales están invirtiendo en cirugías plásticas. Aunque la cirugía plástica no sea para todos, existen varias razones para considerarla. Muchos desean recuperar la apariencia de la juventud, otros quieren mejorar una herencia genética o reparar la piel de los daños causados por la exposición al sol, y algunas mujeres esperan revertir los efectos del embarazo o pérdida de peso. Todos tienen el mismo objetivo final: parecer y sentirse mejores.

![download-19.png](https://clinicabelba.com/wp-content/uploads/elementor/thumbs/descarga-19-1-rkeic1fot282b5drlxhlvn2c0aprivu1c2ubfow2w0.png "download-19.png")

Si usted está preguntándose si necesita una cirugía plástica, la respuesta es: nadie necesita una cirugía plástica— las personas deben querer hacérsela. Aunque sea una decisión compleja, solamente usted puede decidir lo que es mejor para usted misma. Está claro que una mejor apariencia puede hacerla sentir mejor y vivir mejor, elevando su autoestima. En realidad, la cirugía plástica puede ser una de las mayores y más positivas experiencias de su vida, de esa que usted controla. Sin embargo, ella no substituye a una vida saludable, acompañada de una buena alimentación, dieta, práctica de ejercicios y buenas decisiones. No es el secreto de la felicidad. Es apenas parte de un programa de bienestar para maximizar el resultado de una rutina saludable, de vivir bien, de sentirse bien.

![download-20.png](https://clinicabelba.com/wp-content/uploads/elementor/thumbs/descarga-20-1-rkeichey18txsiqk0me9k1163uj05qlh29xkle8dy8.png "download-20.png")

Cuando usted esté delante de una decisión de esa envergadura, es fundamental un periodo de introspección para explorar sus intereses y entender sus motivaciones. La apariencia física, heredada o adquirida, afecta a su autoimagen y la interacción con los otros. Para obtener los resultados más gratificantes de la cirugía plástica, la primera cosa que debe hacer es observarse en el espejo y determinar lo que le incomoda. Defina bien lo que debe ser corregido antes de discutir con la familia, amigos o con el propio cirujano plástico. Y nunca considere un procedimiento exclusivamente para agradar al otro, ni se deje disuadir de su objetivo, a menos que su cirujano afirme que es inadecuado o arriesgado para su caso. A fin de cuentas, quien convive con el problema es usted…

## Risks of plastic surgery

![download-20.png](https://clinicabelba.com/wp-content/uploads/elementor/thumbs/descarga-20-1-rkeichey18txsiqk0me9k1163uj05qlh29xkle8dy8.png "download-20.png")

Since it is elective—and not an emergency operation—plastic surgery has the obligation to minimize risks. To do this, it is enough to choose the doctor judiciously, talk openly about your health and the medications you use, undergo the procedure in an appropriate hospital, and follow pre- and post-operative instructions.

![download-18.png](https://clinicabelba.com/wp-content/uploads/elementor/thumbs/descarga-18-1-rkeibr3gpvtwrfssab0pm7o9h24q67ozmnnz5nbesg.png "download-18.png")

Nevertheless, any surgical procedure carries some risk of complications. Plastic surgery is not an exact science, and sometimes, even when performed by a good professional who will meticulously follow the necessary procedures, the body can respond unexpectedly. Do not blame the doctor in this case—he will likely be as uncomfortable as you are. Fortunately, less severe complications tend to be more frequent, and more serious complications tend to be rarer. Furthermore, most of them end up merely prolonging the recovery period, without compromising the final outcome of the surgery.

![download-19.png](https://clinicabelba.com/wp-content/uploads/elementor/thumbs/descarga-19-1-rkeic1fot282b5drlxhlvn2c0aprivu1c2ubfow2w0.png "download-19.png")

A competent and caring surgeon knows how to resolve a complication and will stay by your side until everything is resolved. In the end, you are the doctor's business card. The main thing in this situation is for the doctor to talk about the causes of the problem and potential solutions honestly and clearly, in order to minimize the patient's anxiety. Be grateful if your doctor has the humility to realize they are unable to correct the problem and refers you to a specialist. In case of uncertainty, the patient should seek the opinion of two or three doctors to ensure they choose the best path and gain confidence.

![download-20.png](https://clinicabelba.com/wp-content/uploads/elementor/thumbs/descarga-20-1-rkeichey18txsiqk0me9k1163uj05qlh29xkle8dy8.png "download-20.png")

Naturally, when considering surgery, no one thinks they might experience a complication. However, risks and complications do happen occasionally, so it's healthy to know a few things about them...

## Plastic surgery before and after

![download-18.png](https://clinicabelba.com/wp-content/uploads/elementor/thumbs/descarga-18-1-rkeibr3gpvtwrfssab0pm7o9h24q67ozmnnz5nbesg.png "download-18.png")

**Important care**

Undergoing surgery is not a normal experience for most of us. Consequently, this stage of treatment is invariably accompanied by some degree of anxiety. Knowing all the stages of treatment can pave the way to providing more comfort and peace of mind. The main thing, however, is to meticulously follow the doctor's instructions to ensure a good outcome.

Ultimately, satisfaction with the results of plastic surgery doesn't just depend on planning. Some citizens, before and after surgery, can prevent potential complications and promote a more satisfying aesthetic outcome. The patient's full collaboration, following the surgeon's instructions, and the actions of healthcare professionals are of great importance in the outcome of the surgery.

![download-18.png](https://clinicabelba.com/wp-content/uploads/elementor/thumbs/descarga-18-1-rkeibr3gpvtwrfssab0pm7o9h24q67ozmnnz5nbesg.png "download-18.png")

Inform your doctor about any chronic illnesses or medications you regularly take, as some medications can increase the risk of bleeding during or after surgery. Therefore, remember to consult the leaflet! Do not use aspirin (acetylsalicylic acid) or any derivatives, arnica, ginkgo biloba, or homeopathic medications containing herbs for 2 weeks before and after surgery. Medications such as Acetaminophen (Tylenol) and Dipyrone (Novalgina) are appropriate substitutes.

![download-19.png](https://clinicabelba.com/wp-content/uploads/elementor/thumbs/descarga-19-1-rkeic1fot282b5drlxhlvn2c0aprivu1c2ubfow2w0.png "download-19.png")

When scheduling your surgery, ensure that the clinic or hospital where the procedure will be performed has the infrastructure required by law. In the unlikely event of complications, the presence of qualified doctors and adequate infrastructure are fundamental for maintaining your health…

![download-20.png](https://clinicabelba.com/wp-content/uploads/elementor/thumbs/descarga-20-1-rkeichey18txsiqk0me9k1163uj05qlh29xkle8dy8.png "download-20.png")
