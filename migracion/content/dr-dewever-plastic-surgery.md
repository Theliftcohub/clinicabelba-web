### Corporal Surgery

**Abdominoplasty:**Abdominoplasty is a surgery aimed at improving the abdomen, removing excess skin and fatty tissue to achieve a flat stomach, firm and with a defined waist. To correct weakness or separation of the abdominal muscles (diastasis recti) a rectus plication is performed. To improve and define the waist, a flank liposuction is performed. Following these procedures, the patient will once again have a firm, flat stomach and a figure-flattering, defined waist – a guaranteed self-esteem boost.

**Liposuction:**
Also known as liposculpture or lipoplasty, involves removing excess localized fat from specific areas of the body through suction. This surgery is suggested for patients who wish to reduce the amount of fat they have in a specific area and who cannot get it to go away with exercise or diet. It is important to understand that lipoaspiration is not suggested for weight loss.

In a liposculpture, a lipoaspiration is performed. After this, the fat that is extracted is treated and injected into another area of the body, such as the breasts, buttocks or face.

**Ultrasonic Rhinoplasty:**
The anatomy of the nose is very complex and varied. For this reason, there is no general rhinoplasty that can be performed since each patient requires their own diagnosis and surgery plan. The most common requests are to correct deviations and size, eliminate bumps or fine-tune the tip of the nose. Ultrasonic rhinoplasty technology allows a much faster recovery that is less painful and which involves less bruising than with a conventional technique.

### Face surgery

**Ultrasonic Rhinoplasty:**
The anatomy of the nose is very complex and varied. For this reason, there is no general rhinoplasty that can be performed since each patient requires their own diagnosis and surgery plan. The most common requests are to correct deviations and size, remove bumps and fine-tune the tip of the nose.

Ultrasonic rhinoplasty technology allows for a much faster, less painful recovery with fewer bruises than a conventional technique.

**Blepharoplasty:**
Blepharoplasty is a type of surgery aimed at improving the look of the patient, providing a younger and more intense appearance. Upper and lower eyelid surgery can be combined or performed separately, depending on each patient’s needs.

**Lifting:**
Each face ages differently, so each patient requires a personalised assessment in order to obtain optimal results. The goal is to restore the vitality of a young face and the firmness of the skin.

**Otoplasty:**
Otoplasty is the name given to surgery that reshapes the ears. Ears are key facial features that give the face harmony: large ears or ears that are very separate or irregular in size create an unbalanced appearance. It is precisely this that this type of surgery corrects.

### Genital Surgery

**Nymphoplasty:**Nymphoplasty is reduction surgery that corrects hypertrophy of the genital labia minora. It is not only performed for aesthetic purposes: surgery may also be necessary for functional reasons, since hypertrophy can cause discomfort in sexual relations, when dressing, playing sports or simply sitting. For these reasons, this type of surgery is being carried out more and more.
