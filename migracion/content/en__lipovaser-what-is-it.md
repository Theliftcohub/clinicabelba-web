Definition

## What is Lipovaser

When we talk about **Lipovaser is a laser-assisted liposuction technique.**, we are referring to an evolution of traditional liposuction. The main difference lies in how the fat is treated before it is extracted: instead of breaking it up solely with the mechanical movement of the cannula, it is applied **ultrasonic energy** to emulsify it, meaning to make it more liquid and easier to remove. This allows for more precise work where the goal is not only to reduce volume but also to sculpt the contour.

![What does Lipovaser consist of: ultrasound-assisted liposuction at Clínica Belba](https://clinicabelba.com/wp-content/uploads/2026/01/cirugia-liposuccion-en-barcelona.webp)

### The difference between Lipovaser and conventional liposuction lies in the technology used to break down and remove fat cells. \*\*Lipovaser\*\* (also known as VASER liposuction) uses ultrasound energy. A specialized probe emits ultrasonic waves that selectively target and emulsify (liquefy) fat cells while leaving surrounding tissues, such as blood vessels and nerves, largely intact. The liquefied fat is then easily suctioned out. This method is known for its precision and ability to create smoother results. \*\*Conventional liposuction\*\*, on the other hand, typically uses a cannula (a thin hollow tube) that is manually moved back and forth to break up fat cells and then suction them out. This method can be more traumatic to the surrounding tissues compared to Lipovaser. Here's a summary of key differences: \* \*\*Technology:\*\* Lipovaser uses ultrasound energy; conventional liposuction uses mechanical force. \* \*\*Fat Breakdown:\*\* Lipovaser emulsifies fat; conventional liposuction breaks it up manually. \* \*\*Tissue Preservation:\*\* Lipovaser is gentler on surrounding tissues; conventional liposuction can be more disruptive. \* \*\*Precision and Smoothness:\*\* Lipovaser often allows for more precise contouring and smoother results. \* \*\*Recovery:\*\* Because Lipovaser can be less traumatic, some patients may experience less bruising and swelling, potentially leading to a slightly faster recovery, though this can vary. \* \*\*Versatility:\*\* Lipovaser can be effective for various body areas, including those with more fibrous fat. In essence, Lipovaser is a more advanced and targeted form of liposuction that utilizes ultrasound technology for potentially better results and a gentler approach.

The difference is in the technology. Lipovaser uses ultrasound to treat the fat before aspirating it, which can facilitate more selective extraction and greater precision in body contouring. That's why, when someone asks **What is VASER liposuction**, the answer shouldn't be limited to "liposuction": it's an ultrasound-assisted liposuction technique, especially interesting when aiming for definition. However, it's not always the best for everyone; in some cases, a [conventional liposuction](https://clinicabelba.com/en/liposuction-barcelona/) It might be enough.

### Why is ultrasound used to treat fat

Ultrasound helps to break down adipocytes and liquefy localized fat. With the fat more separated from the tissue, it can be extracted with greater control. It is usually useful in the abdomen, flanks, back, waist, arms, thighs, double chin, or lumbar region. The goal is not simply to "remove fat," but to do so in a controlled manner to improve contour.

### High definition liposuction.

High-definition liposuction, Lipovaser can work with detail in areas where it's important to mark anatomical lines: abdomen, waist, flanks, or back. It's important to be realistic: definition doesn't solely depend on the machine, but also on anatomy, fat percentage, skin quality, surgeon's experience, and subsequent habits. Lipovaser can help define, but it doesn't create an athletic body on its own if there isn't an adequate foundation.

How does it work

## Lipovaser: What it is and how it works step-by-step

In summary, the procedure follows these steps: assessment, zone design, infiltration, ultrasound application, fat extraction, and post-care compression.

### Medical assessment and design of treatment areas

Before surgery, a body assessment is performed to analyze where localized fat is, if there is sagging or excess skin, if weight is stable, and what outcome is realistic. This step is key because Liposuction High Definition is not suitable for all cases. If the main problem is excess skin, a [Tummy tuck](https://clinicabelba.com/en/tummy-tuck-barcelona/) instead of removing fat.

### Application of ultrasound to liquefy fat

Once the surgery is planned, a fine probe is inserted that emits ultrasonic energy. This energy acts on localized fat, helping to dissolve and separate it from nearby tissues. This phase is what differentiates Lipovaser from other techniques: the fat becomes more prepared for aspiration.

### Liposuction with fine cannulas

Then, the liquefied fat is removed using cannulas. The surgeon works the area to reduce volume, improve contour, and achieve a natural transition between treated and untreated areas. Technique is very important here: a good result is not about aspirating the maximum amount, but about sculpting with judgment.

### Compression garment and first aid

After the intervention, the use of a compression garment is usually indicated, which helps control inflammation, promote tissue adaptation, and improve recovery. Lymphatic drainage, check-ups, and specific guidelines depending on the treated area may also be recommended.

Keep in mind

The surgical plan and the areas to be treated are decided individually during a consultation, based on your anatomy and your goals. No technique offers a guaranteed result: a prior medical assessment determines the indication.

### Contour improvement for the abdomen, flanks, back, and arms

The most common areas are the abdomen, flanks, and lower back, because these are areas where localized fat tends to be resistant. It can also be used on the arms, thighs, knees, or double chin, provided there is a good indication.

Full contour

## What is Lipovaser 360

The **Lipovaser 360** It is a body contouring technique that works on the overall torso, not just isolated areas. It usually includes the abdomen, flanks, waist, and lower back, although the exact approach depends on each patient.

### Difference between Liposuction by areas and 360 Liposuction

A Lipovaser by zones treats specific areas (abdomen, arms, flanks, or thighs). Lipovaser 360 aims to reshape the entire contour around the torso, which can offer a more harmonious result because it doesn't just work "from the front," but also the waist, back, and the body's lateral transition.

### A 360 liposculpture makes sense when you want to address fat deposits in multiple areas of your body simultaneously to achieve a more balanced and contoured physique. This procedure is ideal for individuals who are close to their ideal weight but struggle with stubborn fat in areas like the abdomen, flanks, back, and sometimes the arms or thighs, which don't respond well to diet and exercise. It's also beneficial for improving overall body shape and creating a smoother silhouette by treating the entire midsection.

It can make sense when there is localized fat around the torso, a more defined waist is desired, there is good skin elasticity, weight is stable, and there is no significant excess skin. If there is significant abdominal laxity or loose skin, it may be better to consider abdominoplasty.

Indication

## Liposuction is a surgical procedure that removes unwanted fat deposits. It is typically performed on individuals who are close to their ideal body weight but have stubborn pockets of fat that do not respond to diet and exercise. Ideal candidates for liposuction are generally in good health, do not smoke, and have realistic expectations about the results. Here are some key characteristics of a good candidate for liposuction: \* \*\*Close to their ideal body weight:\*\* Liposuction is not a weight-loss solution. It is best for contouring the body and removing localized fat. \* \*\*Good skin elasticity:\*\* When fat is removed, the skin needs to be able to retract and conform to the new body shape. Good skin elasticity helps achieve smoother results. \* \*\*Healthy and fit:\*\* General good health is important for any surgical procedure. Patients should not have significant underlying medical conditions that could interfere with healing or increase surgical risks. \* \*\*Non-smokers:\*\* Smoking can impair healing and increase the risk of complications. Surgeons often require patients to quit smoking for a period before and after surgery. \* \*\*Realistic expectations:\*\* Candidates should understand that liposuction can improve body shape but will not eliminate cellulite, stretch marks, or sagging skin. They should discuss their goals openly with their surgeon to ensure their expectations are achievable. \* \*\*Stable weight:\*\* It's recommended to be at a stable weight for at least six months to a year before considering liposuction. Significant weight fluctuations after the procedure can affect the results. \* \*\*Specific areas of concern:\*\* Candidates usually have specific areas they want to address, such as the abdomen, hips, thighs, arms, or chin. \*\*Who might NOT be a good candidate?\*\* \* Individuals who are significantly overweight or obese. \* People with poor skin elasticity. \* Those with medical conditions like heart disease, diabetes, or compromised immune systems. \* Pregnant or breastfeeding women. \* Individuals who smoke or have recently quit. \* People with unrealistic expectations about the outcome. It is crucial to consult with a board-certified plastic surgeon to determine if liposuction is the right procedure for your individual needs and goals. They will assess your health, discuss your expectations, and recommend the most appropriate treatment plan.

Not everyone is a good candidate, and this part is essential to avoid unrealistic expectations.

### Patients near their ideal weight

The ideal candidate is usually close to their stable weight but has localized fat that doesn't respond as expected to diet and exercise. If there is significant overweight, it's advisable to address the weight first before considering Lipovaser.

### Good skin elasticity

The skin must be able to adapt to the new contour. If there is a lot of sagging or excess skin, removing fat can worsen the appearance because the skin may droop more. In those cases, the solution is not Liposonix, but rather to consider surgery to remove the excess skin.

### When it is not the best option

* When there is obesity or the goal is to lose weight;
* when there is a lot of sagging or excess skin;
* when expectations are unrealistic;
* when there are uncontrolled diseases;
* When postoperative care cannot be followed.

The medical evaluation is what determines if Lipovaser makes sense or if another alternative is advisable.

Recovery

## Lipovaser recovery typically involves some discomfort, swelling, and bruising for the first few days. Most patients can return to light activities within 1-2 weeks, while more strenuous exercise may need to be avoided for 4-6 weeks. It's important to wear compression garments as recommended by your doctor to aid in healing and reduce swelling. Many patients report seeing initial results quickly, with the final outcome becoming more apparent as swelling subsides over several months.

Recovery depends on the areas treated, the amount of fat removed, the type of anesthesia, and the patient's individual response.

### Inflammation, bruising, and normal discomfort

After liposuction, it is normal to notice swelling, bruising, discomfort, a feeling of tightness, changes in sensitivity, or fluid retention. These symptoms usually improve progressively, but the process requires patience.

### Use of a girdle or compression garment

The compression garment or garment helps control inflammation and promote tissue adaptation. Its use should follow the medical team's instructions, and it should not be removed prematurely without authorization.

### Drainages, revisions, and return to activity

In some cases, lymphatic drainage is recommended, and postoperative check-ups are important. The return to work depends on the type of activity, and sports should be resumed gradually and always with medical authorization.

During the surgery, anesthesia is used, so it shouldn't hurt. Afterward, there may be discomfort, swelling, bruising, or tenderness, which are usually managed by following medical instructions. The intensity varies depending on the areas treated and each person's response.

When are the results of Lipovaser visible?

The first changes can be noticed when the swelling subsides, but the final result usually takes several months to be fully appreciated. Initially, there is normal swelling, retention, and discomfort, so it is advisable to assess the result with perspective and patience.
