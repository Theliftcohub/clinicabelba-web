## A tummy tuck is recommended when a person has excess skin and fat in the abdominal area that cannot be removed through diet and exercise alone. This often occurs after significant weight loss or pregnancy, which can cause the skin to stretch and lose elasticity. It's also recommended for individuals who are in good overall health and have realistic expectations about the results.

It is usually recommended when there is:

* Loose skin after pregnancy
* Sagging skin after significant weight loss
* Flabby abdomen
* Muscle diastasis
* Difficulty recovering abdominal contour with diet and exercise

## Types of tummy tuck: tummy tuck, mini tummy tuck and liposuction

Although they are sometimes confused, they are not the same.

### **Abdominoplasty**

Corrects excess skin, sagging, and can repair muscle diastasis.

### **Mini tummy tuck**

It is indicated when the problem is concentrated in the lower abdomen and there is less excess skin.

### **Liposuction**

It's effective for eliminating localized fat, but it doesn't correct skin laxity or muscle separation.

This difference is key because it also changes the **Final treatment price**.

## An abdominoplasty is worth considering when: \* You have excess abdominal skin and fat that doesn't respond to diet and exercise. \* Your abdominal muscles are separated (diastasis recti), often a result of pregnancy or significant weight fluctuations. \* You have sagging or loose skin in your abdomen due to aging, pregnancy, or weight loss. \* You are unhappy with the appearance of your abdomen and it significantly impacts your self-esteem. \* You are in good overall health and not planning future pregnancies, as this can affect the results. \* You are at a stable, healthy weight.

Abdominoplasty is usually worth it when the main problem is not just fat, but also **spare skin**, flabbiness or weakness of the abdominal wall. In these cases, liposuction alone usually does not produce the same result.

It can be especially useful in patients who:

* Have they had pregnancies
* They have lost a lot of weight
* They have abdominal diastasis
* They want to get a firmer, flatter stomach.

### The recovery from a tummy tuck typically involves several stages. Immediately after surgery, you'll likely experience swelling, bruising, and discomfort, and will need to wear a compression garment. You'll be advised to rest and avoid strenuous activity. Pain medication will be prescribed to manage discomfort. The first week is usually the most challenging. You'll need assistance with daily activities and may have drainage tubes in place to remove excess fluid. Most patients can resume light activities like walking around the house by the end of the first week, which helps prevent blood clots. Over the next several weeks, swelling and bruising will gradually subside. You'll be able to gradually increase your activity level, but heavy lifting and intense exercise should still be avoided for at least 4-6 weeks, or as advised by your surgeon. Your incision will need to be kept clean and dry. Full recovery can take several months. While you'll start to see results relatively quickly, the final outcome will become more apparent as the swelling completely resolves and your body fully heals. It's important to follow your surgeon's post-operative instructions closely to ensure the best possible results and a smooth recovery. This includes attending all follow-up appointments.

Abdominoplasty recovery takes longer than less invasive body procedures. After surgery, you can usually expect:

* Inflammation
* Abdominal binder
* Temporary movement restriction
* Need to use a postoperative girdle
* Progressive medical follow-up

Rejoining your routine depends on each patient, the type of surgery, and postoperative progress.

## Frequently Asked Questions about Abdominoplasty Cost

### How much does an abdominoplasty cost?

The price varies depending on the type of [plastic surgery](https://clinicabelba.com/en/), the complexity of the case, the clinical presentation, and what the budget includes. The best way to know is with a personalized assessment.

### What is the price of a tummy tuck in Barcelona?

In Barcelona, the indicative range is usually between **€5,500 and €8,500**, though it may be higher if combined with liposuction or more complex muscle repair.

### What is included in the price of a tummy tuck?

It usually includes consultation, surgical planning, medical fees, anesthesia, operating room, and follow-up appointments, although it depends on the clinic.

### Is a tummy tuck more expensive than liposuction?

In general, yes. A tummy tuck is a more complete surgery because it can involve skin removal and muscle repair, not just fat removal.

### What is the difference between a tummy tuck and a mini tummy tuck?

Mini-abdominoplasty treats milder, more localized cases in the lower abdomen. A full abdominoplasty corrects more excess skin and can address muscle diastasis.

### How many kilograms are removed in an abdominoplasty?

A tummy tuck is not a weight loss surgery, but rather to improve abdominal contour. The amount of tissue removed varies depending on each case.

### A tummy tuck with liposuction is recommended when excess fat and skin are present, particularly in the abdomen, and the abdominal muscles may be separated or relaxed. It's often considered after significant weight loss or pregnancy, when skin elasticity has diminished.

When, in addition to excess skin, there is localized fat that needs to be reshaped for a more harmonious result.

### Does abdominoplasty leave a scar?

Yes. Like any surgery of this type, it leaves a scar, although it is planned to be as discreet as possible under underwear or a bikini.
